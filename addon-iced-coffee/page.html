the brown sugar babe

☕ ~70 cal at home⏱ 5 min🌱 dairy-free option

you need

  • 2 shots espresso (or ½ cup strong cold brew)
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • pinch of cinnamon
  • ice
  • ½ cup unsweetened oat milk

make it at home

  1. shake espresso, sugar, cinnamon + ice in a jar for 10 sec
  2. pour into a glass of ice
  3. top with oat milk. done.

ordering out? say this 👇

"can i get a grande iced shaken espresso with oat milk, just 1 pump of brown sugar, and cinnamon on top?"

📌 protein hack: swap half the oat milk for a vanilla protein shake = +15g protein
same vibe, way less syrup. your barista won't even blink.
