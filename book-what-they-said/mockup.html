A GUIDED JOURNAL
Before you begin

I didn't write this book.

Thousands of strangers did. One question was asked online — “What's something a family member said that you'll never fully forgive?” — and people answered with things they had never said out loud.

I read every answer. I kept the words that stayed. And under each one, I wrote what that person deserved to hear instead.

You don't have to read this in order. Open it anywhere. Write, or don't. Put it down when it gets heavy — that's allowed too.

A gentle note: some pages touch on grief, rejection and hurtful family moments. Stories have been shortened and changed so no one can be identified. If this brings up something big, please reach out to someone you trust or a support line — there's a list at the back.
