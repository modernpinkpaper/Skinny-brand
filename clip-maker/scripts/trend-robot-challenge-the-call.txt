In baseball now, a robot can overrule the umpire.

A player doesn't have to accept the call.
He taps his helmet. He asks for another look.
And more than half the time, the call gets changed.

Now think about the calls you've accepted.
The voice that said you're too much.
The voice that said you're behind.
The voice that said you should have known better.
You never asked for a second look.
You just took the verdict and walked back to the dugout.


But that voice was only one opinion.
It was tired. It was scared. It was fast.
That's not the truth. That's a call.
And a call can be challenged.

Ask for the replay.
Look at what really happened, slowly, with kind eyes.
Maybe you weren't lazy. You were exhausted.
Maybe you weren't weak. You were carrying too much.
Maybe you weren't failing. You were learning.

Not every call against you is correct.
Some are just loud.
You're allowed to slow them down.


So tap your helmet, and ask for another look.
You've earned the replay.
