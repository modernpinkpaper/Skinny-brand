Some people don't miss you.
They miss having a door they can walk through.

You know the type.
They disappear when life is easy.
They come back when they're lonely.
They say sorry, but only when it's cold outside.
And you open the door, because you're kind.

You call it patience.
You call it loyalty.
You call it being the bigger person.


But a door that's always open isn't love. It's access.
And access is something a person earns.

Think about what it cost you.
The nights you showed up tired.
The effort nobody thanked.
The way you got smaller to keep the peace.

That's not coldness. That's not bitterness.
That's a closed door with a quiet sign on it.
You can still wish them well.
You can still carry no hate.

Kindness doesn't mean unlimited entry.
Love shouldn't feel like a revolving door.
Some people are visitors. Some people are home.
Learn the difference.


Close it gently, and let it stay closed.
