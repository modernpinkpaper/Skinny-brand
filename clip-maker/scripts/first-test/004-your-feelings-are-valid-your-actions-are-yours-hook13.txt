Just because you feel something doesn't mean you did something wrong.
A feeling is information, not a confession.
Anger shows up when a line got crossed.
Jealousy shows up when you want what you don't have.
Neither one makes you a bad person.

Here's the part most people skip.
You don't choose the feeling.
You choose the next five minutes.
That's where you actually live.

So you feel angry, and you name it.
You feel jealous, and you stay curious instead of cruel.
You feel hurt, and you say it out loud instead of going cold.
That's the whole skill. Nothing fancy.


But when you decide a feeling is shameful, you bury it.
And buried things don't disappear. They leak.
They leak as silence, as sarcasm, as slammed doors.
So let it be there. Feel it fully.
Then ask what it's asking you for.

That's not weakness. That's control.
Your feelings are not a verdict on who you are.
Your actions are.


Feel everything. Own what you do next.
