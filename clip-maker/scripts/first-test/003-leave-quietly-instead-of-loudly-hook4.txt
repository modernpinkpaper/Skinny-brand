You can't wait for permission just because you loved them more.

Leaving feels like it needs a scene.
A big talk. A reason they accept.
So you stay, rehearsing a speech you never give.

You keep the peace by going quiet.
You check their mood before your own.
You tell yourself next month will be calmer.

But calm never comes when you announce it.
Announcing gives them time to talk you out.


So you move slowly instead.
You open your own account.
You keep copies of what matters.
You tell one person you trust.
You save a little every week.

That's not sneaking. That's safety.
That's not cowardice. That's a plan.

Some doors you close with a speech.
Some you close with a bag already packed.
You don't owe anyone a dramatic ending.
You owe yourself a quiet beginning.


Leave slowly. Leave prepared.
And don't wait for applause on your way out.
