Just because you understand your pain doesn't mean you've healed it.
You can name the wound.
You can trace it back to the exact year.
And still wake up carrying it.

You've done the reading.
You know the words for what happened to you.
You sit in that chair and explain your own patterns.
The room goes quiet.

You hear that you're doing everything right.
That your coping is what they'd suggest anyway.
That maybe you don't even need to be here.
And you smile, because it sounds like a compliment.


But insight is not the same as relief.
That's not healing. That's a really good explanation.
You didn't get this self aware because you were wise.
You got this self aware because no one was coming.

Understanding kept you safe.
It never held you.
Some people need answers.
You need someone to stay.


Stop performing how fine you are and let someone in.
