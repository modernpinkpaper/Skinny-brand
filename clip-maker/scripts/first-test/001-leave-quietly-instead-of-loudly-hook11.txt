Always trust the quiet exit.

The one nobody sees coming.
No big speech. No final fight.
Just you, getting free.

You keep waiting for a moment that feels big enough.
A reason they can't argue with.
Permission, from the one person who will never give it.

So you stay.
You rehearse the conversation in the shower.
You pack a bag in your head at 2 a.m.
You tell yourself you'll go when it gets worse.


But leaving was never supposed to be a performance.
That's not weakness. That's strategy.
Quiet isn't cowardly. Quiet is safe.

Save a little money. Copy the documents.
Tell one person you trust.
Make the boring plan.
Then move, slowly, like nothing is happening.

You don't owe anyone a dramatic goodbye.
You don't need them to agree that you were hurting.
Some doors you slam. Some you just walk through.


The loud exits get remembered.
The quiet ones get to start over.

So plan it. Then go quietly.
