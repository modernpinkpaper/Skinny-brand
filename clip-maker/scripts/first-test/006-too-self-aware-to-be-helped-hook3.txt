The saddest part is you understand yourself perfectly and it still hurts.

You can name the wound.
You can trace it back to the exact year.
You know why you flinch when voices get loud.

You sit in the chair and explain your own patterns.
The therapist nods and says you're already doing the work.
You walk out feeling smart and still empty.
You journal about the same thing for three years.
You give friends advice you've never once followed.


But insight is not the same as healing.
That's not recovery. That's a very clean cage.
You describe the pain so well that nobody reaches for you.

Being understandable is not the same as being held.
Knowing why you're drowning won't teach you to swim.
Some people need answers. You already have them.
What you need is someone who stays past the explanation.


So stop proving you're fine. Let someone help you anyway.
