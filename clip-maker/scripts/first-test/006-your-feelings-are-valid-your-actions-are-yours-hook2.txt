There's a theory that says you never choose the feeling, only the next move.

You can't decide to stop being angry.
You can't talk yourself out of jealousy.
The feeling shows up before you do.

So you feel it and then you feel guilty for feeling it.
And guilt makes people do one of two things.
Bury it, or throw it at someone.

You go quiet for three days instead of saying you were hurt.
You check their profile again and hate yourself after.
You snap at someone who had nothing to do with it.


But a feeling is information, not instruction.
Anger is not cruelty. Jealousy is not betrayal.
They are just signals telling you something matters.

So name it before you act on it.
Say it out loud, even alone. I am jealous. I am angry.
Then wait. Ten minutes is usually enough.
Then choose the action you would defend tomorrow.

Your feelings are not the problem.
You are not a bad person for having them.


Feel everything. Act on purpose.
