All of us are angry, jealous, bitter sometimes.

Feelings are not choices.
They show up before you agree to them.
You cannot vote them out.
So stop apologizing for having a heart.

Here is the line most people never learn.
Feeling is weather. Doing is a decision.

You felt jealous when your friend won.
That's normal.
You felt rage when someone talked over you.
That's normal.
You felt relief when they finally failed.
Still normal.


But what you do next belongs to you.
That's not the same thing. That's the whole thing.

So feel it. Name it out loud.
Wait ten seconds before you answer.
Then pick your next sentence on purpose.

Guilt tells you the feeling is the crime.
It isn't.
The crime is what you throw.

No feeling is wrong. Some actions are.
You can be furious and still be kind.
You can be jealous and still clap.


You are not a bad person for feeling it.
You're a grown one for choosing what to do with it.
