You can't announce your exit just because you want it to be fair.

Some doors have to close softly.
Not because you're sneaky.
Because loud goodbyes give them one more chance to talk you out of it.

You've already tried the honest way.
The long talk that went in circles.
The promise that lasted a week.
The apology that cost you more than the fight.

So you stopped explaining.
You started noticing.
How much you have saved.
Who would pick up at midnight.
Which box of your things still matters.


But that isn't cold.
That's not betrayal. That's preparation.
You are not running away. You are walking out on purpose.

Make the quiet moves first.
Small steps. No speeches.
Some people deserve a conversation. Some only need a closed door.
You don't owe a warning to something that never warned you.


Plan it slowly, then leave without asking anyone if it's allowed.
