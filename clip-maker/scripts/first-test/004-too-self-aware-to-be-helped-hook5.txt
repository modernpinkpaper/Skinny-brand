The reason you're so good at explaining your pain
is because you had to survive it alone.
You learned the words for it.
You can name the wound in perfect detail.

So you sit in the chair and do their work.
You explain your childhood like a case study.
You list your coping skills and they nod.
Someone calls you very self aware.
And you take it like a compliment.


But understanding is not healing.
That's not progress. That's a smart place to hide.
You built the map so nobody could lead you.
Insight is not the same as change.
Knowing why you flinch doesn't stop the flinch.
What you never had was someone holding it with you.


So say the messy part out loud.
The part you can't analyze yet.
Let someone help the you who hasn't figured it out.
