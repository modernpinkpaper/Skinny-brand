People who leave for good don't announce it. They prepare.
They stop asking for permission.
They stop waiting to be understood.
They stop explaining the same pain to someone who keeps forgetting it.

You already know this feeling.
You've rehearsed the conversation in the shower.
You've kept the peace all week so nothing would blow up.
You've told yourself you'd go once you finally had the right words.


But the words were never the problem.
The plan was.
So build it quietly.
Open the account nobody asks about.
Save what you can, a little at a time.
Keep your documents somewhere safe, and one person who knows.

Move slow, and move anyway.
That's not sneaking. That's safety.
That's not cold. That's care for yourself.
You don't owe anyone a final speech.
Some doors need slamming. Some just need closing.
A loud exit needs their permission. A quiet one only needs yours.


Start getting ready long before you're ready to go.
