There are some people in this world who leave without ever announcing it.

No big speech.
No final fight.
No trying to be understood one last time.

Because somewhere along the way, you learned something.
Every time you explained yourself, it got twisted.
Every time you said you were unhappy, it became your fault.

So you stopped explaining.
You started planning.

You opened the account nobody knew about.
You kept the job you were told to quit.
You saved a little more each month and said nothing.


But that's not sneaking around.
That's not weakness.
That's someone finally choosing their own safety.

Loud leaving gives them something to argue with.
Quiet leaving gives you a door.

You don't need their permission to go.
You don't need them to agree that it hurt.
Some doors were never meant to be slammed.


So build the plan slowly.
Move gently.
And when it's ready, walk out quietly.
You were never required to stay and be understood.
