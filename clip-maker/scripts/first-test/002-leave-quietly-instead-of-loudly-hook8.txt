People who leave for good don't slam the door.
They pack quietly.
Because the loud exit feels like justice.
The final speech. The last word.
The moment they finally understand you.

But that moment never comes.
You explain, and they argue.
You cry, and they promise.
You stay another year.

So you go small instead.
You open your own account.
You keep copies of what matters.
You tell one safe person the truth.
You move a little at a time.


That's not cowardice. That's a plan.
And a plan is the thing that actually gets you out.
You don't need their permission to go.
You don't need them to agree it was bad.

Some doors close softly.
Some freedom is built in silence.
A quiet exit is not a weak one.
It's the one that works.
You were never required to announce your escape.


Make the plan. Then make the move. Quietly.
