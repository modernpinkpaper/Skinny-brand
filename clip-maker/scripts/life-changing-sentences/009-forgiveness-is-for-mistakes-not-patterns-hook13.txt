Just because someone says sorry doesn't mean they plan to change.

You keep waiting for the apology to turn into action.
And it never does.
So you forgive again, and call it love.

But hope is not evidence.
You are watching the same story on repeat.

The same fight, three months later, with new words.
The same promise, made softly, at two in the morning.
The same tears from you, the same silence from them.


Forgiveness is for mistakes.
A mistake happens once.
What you are forgiving is a pattern.
And a pattern is not an accident. It's a choice.

You can forgive someone completely and still walk away.
That's not bitterness. That's clarity.
Forgiveness sets you free. Boundaries keep you safe.
You need both.

When someone keeps showing you who they are, believe them the first time.
Not the tenth.
Because staying loyal to a pattern is not loyalty. It's leaving yourself behind.


Accept people exactly as they are, then put them where they belong.
