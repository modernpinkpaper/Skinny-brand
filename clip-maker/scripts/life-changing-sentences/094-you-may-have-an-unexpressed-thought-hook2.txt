There's a theory that says you're allowed
to have a thought you never say.
Not every honest thing needs an audience.
You can notice it, feel it, and let it pass.
Silence isn't lying. It's choosing.

Think of the last time you told the truth
and watched someone shrink.
The comment about her body, said for her own good.
The venting that traveled into rooms you never entered.
The argument you won, and lost the person anyway.


But here's the practical part.
Before you speak, ask three questions.
Is it true? Is it kind?
Does it help right now?
If it fails two of them, hold it.

Truth without care is just cruelty with manners.
Care without truth is just a soft lie.
You need both, or neither one counts.
Being right is easy.
Being effective takes work.

Most fights aren't about facts.
They're about timing.
So keep the thought. Write it down.
Sit with it for one night.


And if it still matters tomorrow, say it gently.
