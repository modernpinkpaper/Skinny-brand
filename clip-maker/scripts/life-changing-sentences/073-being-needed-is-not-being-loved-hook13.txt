Just because someone needs you doesn't mean they love you.

Needing you is about what you give.
Loving you is about who you are.
And you learned early to keep giving.
So you made yourself useful, over and over.

You're the one who fixes things.
You're the one who lends the money.
You're the one who answers at 2 a.m.
You say yes before you even check how tired you are.
You hold the whole family together with your hands.


But you've never been allowed to be empty.
You don't know if you'd still be wanted with nothing left to give.
That's not love. That's a job.

You can't be the glue for something that keeps breaking itself.
Their choices were never your responsibility.
If you have to earn your place, it isn't a place.
It's a rent you pay forever.
Some people miss you. Some people just miss the help.
Love doesn't need you useful. It just wants you here.


So put something down this week, and see who stays.
