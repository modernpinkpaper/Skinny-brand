How can you live freely when you check every word before you say it?

Fear of looking stupid runs more of your life than you admit.
You call it being careful.
It's really being small.

You delete the message twice before sending it.
You don't dance because someone might film you.
You stay quiet in the room even when you know the answer.


But think about the last time you were actually embarrassed.
Someone thought you were cringe for about four seconds.
Then they went back to worrying about themselves.
That's not a punishment. That's the whole price.

They forget by Tuesday. You remember for years.
Their opinion is a passing thought. Your fear is a full-time job.
Some cages have locks. Some you just stand inside.

You were never trapped by what they think.
You were trapped by how hard you tried to control it.


So be the one who tried. Be a little too much. Be cringe.
It costs someone a moment. It gives you your whole life back.
