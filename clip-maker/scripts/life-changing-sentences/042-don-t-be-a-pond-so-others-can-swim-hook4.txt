You can't turn into a pond just because someone never learned to swim.
But you do it anyway.
You soften your voice in rooms you helped build.
You laugh at yourself first, so nobody else gets the chance.
And you call it being humble.

You hide the win because someone else is having a hard week.
You water down the dream so the table stays comfortable.
You let someone correct you when you were right.
You apologize for taking up space you actually earned.


But that's not humility. That's disappearing politely.
Humility knows what it is. Shrinking forgets.

Self-deprecation is not modesty. It's a tax you pay to be liked.
Confidence is quiet. Insecurity is loud.
And their loudness was never your job to carry.
Clothes are cut to fit you. You were never cut to fit them.
Some people want you smaller. Some people want you free.


So walk into every room like you were sent there.
The ocean doesn't apologize for being deep.
