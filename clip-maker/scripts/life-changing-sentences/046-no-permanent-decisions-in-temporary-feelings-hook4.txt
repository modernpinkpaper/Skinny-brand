You can't burn down your whole life just because today went wrong.

A bad hour is not a bad job.
A bad week is not a bad person.
Feelings pass. Consequences stay.

Think of the text you sent at midnight.
The job you almost walked out of.
The friend you nearly deleted over one sentence.

So here's the rule.
No permanent decisions in temporary feelings.
When you're angry, tired, hungry or humiliated,
your brain is not planning.
It's defending.

Write the resignation. Don't send it.
Type the message. Save it in your notes.
Make the decision. Give it three days.


But this isn't about staying forever.
It's about choosing on a calm day, not a cruel one.
If it still feels wrong when you're rested and steady,
then it's not a mood. It's an answer.

Quit on a good day.
Leave when you're clear, not when you're bleeding.
Not every urge is a sign.
Some are just exhaustion wearing a costume.


Sleep first. Decide second.
Your future self is the one paying for tonight.
