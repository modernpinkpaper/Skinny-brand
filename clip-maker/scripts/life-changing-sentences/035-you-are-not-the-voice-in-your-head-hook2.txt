There's a theory that says you are not the voice in your head.
You are the one who hears it.

That voice talks all day.
It calls you lazy.
It calls you too much.
And because it uses your own voice, you believe it.

Notice when it happens.
It gets loud when you're tired.
It gets loud after someone leaves you on read.
It gets loud at 2 a.m. when nothing can be fixed.


But volume is not proof.
A thought is not a fact.
It's a guess.

Here's the practice.
When the thought comes, name it.
Say, a thought is here.
Don't obey it.
Then ask one question.
Would you say this to a friend?
If the answer is no, it isn't truth.
It's a habit.

Your feelings are real.
That doesn't make them right.
Just don't take orders from them.

You are not the narrator.
You are the one listening.


So stop treating every thought as news.
Let it pass, and choose your next action anyway.
