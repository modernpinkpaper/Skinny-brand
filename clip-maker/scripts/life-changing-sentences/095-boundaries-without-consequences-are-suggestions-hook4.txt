You can't call it a boundary
just because you said it out loud.

A boundary isn't a sentence.
It's an action.
If nothing changes when it gets crossed,
you didn't set a limit.
You made a request.

You told them not to call after midnight.
You still pick up.
You said you needed the money back.
You never brought it up again.
You asked them to stop joking about your body.
You laughed anyway.


But they're not confused.
They're comfortable.
That's not a misunderstanding. That's information.
Every time you let it slide,
you taught them it was fine.

So say it once, plainly.
Then decide what you will do,
not what they should feel.
Leave the room. End the call. Go home early.
Do it the first time, calmly, without a speech.
You don't have to explain it twice.

A limit with no cost is just a wish.
You don't need them to agree with you.
You need you to follow through.


Say it once.
Then let your actions repeat it.
