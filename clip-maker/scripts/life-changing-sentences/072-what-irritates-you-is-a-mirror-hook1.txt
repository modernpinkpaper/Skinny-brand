There's a psychology rule called projection.
It says the traits that irritate you most in other people
are usually the traits you refuse to see in yourself.
You don't see people as they are.
You see them as you are.

Think about the last person who annoyed you.
The loud one who needs every eye in the room.
The friend who always plays the victim.
The coworker who brags a little too hard.
Now ask yourself what part of that you were taught to hide.


But irritation is never random.
Something in you recognized it first.
That's not weakness. That's information.

So try this.
When someone gets under your skin, stop and name the exact trait.
Then finish this sentence honestly.
I hate seeing this because I know it lives in me too.

Some accusations are confessions.
The way you talk about people describes you, not them.
Judgement is a map of your own hidden rooms.


Next time someone irritates you, get curious before you get angry.
