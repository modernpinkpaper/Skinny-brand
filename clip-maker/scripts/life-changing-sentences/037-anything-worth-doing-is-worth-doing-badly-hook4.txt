You can't skip being a beginner just because you hate being bad at things.

Perfectionism doesn't feel like fear.
It feels like having standards.
So you wait until you're ready, and ready never comes.
And the hobby you wanted quietly stays a plan.

The guitar in the corner you never touch.
The language app you deleted after one bad lesson.
The drawing you hid before anyone saw it.


But skill isn't the ticket in.
Embarrassment is.
Nobody learns something they already know how to do.

So try this.
Aim for a time, not a result.
Fifteen bad minutes counts.
Do the worst version on purpose.
Then make it slightly less bad tomorrow.

Keep the first ten attempts private if you need to.
And count the days you showed up, not the quality.
Strive for excellence, not perfection.

Failing sometimes means you finally picked something real.
Your tenth try needs your first try.
You don't have to be good at it to be allowed to love it.


So start it badly.
Badly still counts.
