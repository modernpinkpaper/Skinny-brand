There's a theory that says every storm runs out of rain.
Not because you begged it to stop.
Because no storm has enough to last forever.

But when you're in it, you don't believe that.
You start reading this chapter like it's the ending.
You start calling a bad season your whole life.

You check your phone for news that doesn't come.
You do the same small things again, quietly.
You lie awake counting how long this has lasted.
You tell people you're fine because explaining feels too heavy.


But nothing has ended yet.
It hasn't worked out, so it isn't the end.

You're not stuck. You're in the middle.
The middle always feels like forever.
What broke open in you let some light in.
Some dark places are graves. Some are soil.
Sometimes when you think you've been buried, you've been planted.

Tomorrow can be better. But today still has to happen first.
So don't decide your whole story on the worst page of it.


Stay through today. The rain is running out.
