You can't call it stuck just because you never chose it out loud.

Staying is a choice too.
It just doesn't feel like one.
Because nobody makes you sign anything.
You just wake up and do the same day again.

You say you'll leave after the holidays.
You say you'll talk to them when things calm down.
You say next month, next year, when you're stronger.
And another Sunday night arrives, exactly like the last one.


But nothing is holding you there but the hope it fixes itself.
That's not patience. That's permission.
You're not waiting for change. You're voting against it.

Every day you don't move is a vote for this.
People hate change. They hate the way things are more.
The room doesn't change because you got used to the smell.
You can't heal in the place that keeps hurting you.
Some doors were wrong from the start.
Walking back out isn't failure. It's honesty.


So change one small thing today, before your life decides for you.
