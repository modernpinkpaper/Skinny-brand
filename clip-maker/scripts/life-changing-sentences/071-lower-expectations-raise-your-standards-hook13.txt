Just because someone showed you their best once
doesn't mean that's who they are.

An expectation is a guess about a person.
A standard is a rule about you.
Most people have those backwards.

You expect the good week to come back.
You expect the apology you've imagined.
You expect them to finally ask how you are.
And then you accept whatever they hand you.


But hoping harder has never changed anyone.
That's not patience.
That's a guess you keep paying for.

So do this.
Watch what someone does for a month,
and call that their ceiling.
Not their bad day. Not their best day.
Their normal.

Then write down three things you need.
Honesty. Effort. Consistency.
If someone can't meet those, that's information,
not a reason to lower the list.

Expect people to be who they've been.
Refuse to be treated below your line.
Lower the guess. Raise the rule.


Accept everyone exactly as they are,
and place them exactly where they belong.
