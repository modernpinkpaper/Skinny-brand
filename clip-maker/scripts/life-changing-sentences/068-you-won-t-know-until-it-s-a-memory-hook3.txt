The saddest part is you were happy and you just didn't know.

You were standing in the best year of your life,
waiting for it to begin.
Waiting for the better job, the better body, the better version of you.
So you were there, but you weren't really there.

The late night kitchen with people who don't call anymore.
The drive home with the windows down and nowhere to be.
An ordinary Tuesday you would give anything to walk back into.
None of it felt special. All of it was.


But you weren't missing the good part.
You were early.
It's not that the moment wasn't beautiful.
It's that your mind was somewhere else.

Memory isn't magic. It's attention, paid too late.
Nothing turns golden. It always was.
Some days are events. Some days are quiet gifts.
And no one warns you when the good part starts.


So look up while it's still happening.
The memory you'll miss the most is being made right now.
