All of us are doing this for the first time.

The man who cut you off in traffic.
The woman who snapped at you in line.
Your mother, who says the wrong thing
every single time.

None of them woke up with a plan to ruin you.
They're just tired.
They're just clumsy with their own pain.

Think about the last time you hurt someone.
You probably didn't mean it.
You were rushed, or scared, or empty.
You didn't intend harm. You caused it anyway.


So maybe most people are not your enemy.
Maybe they're just bad at being human today.
That's not malice. That's practice.

Anger feels like power. It's actually weight.
You carry it home. They don't.

Being gentle isn't letting people win.
It's refusing to spend your day on fire.
And kindness stays longer than you think.

Some people need a lesson. Most just need a little room.


So give them the room.
Not because they earned it.
Because you're tired of carrying the rest.
