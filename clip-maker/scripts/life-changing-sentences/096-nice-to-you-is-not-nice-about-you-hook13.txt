Just because someone is nice to you doesn't mean they're nice about you.
Kindness to your face costs nothing.
What people say when you leave the room is the real thing.
And you rarely get to hear it.

The warm hello that goes cold the second you turn around.
The friend who laughs with you, then repeats your secret.
The coworker who smiles, then leaves your name out.
The text that's sweet while the group chat says something else.


But you're not crazy for feeling the gap.
That's not paranoia. That's your body noticing a pattern.

Charm is easy. Consistency isn't.
Watch how they treat people who can do nothing for them.
Watch what stays the same when you're not around.

Some people are kind. Some are only polite.
Polite is a costume. Kind is a habit.
You don't need revenge. You need better eyes.


When someone shows you who they are, believe them.
Live so well that bad words about you sound false.
