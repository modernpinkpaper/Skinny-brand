You can't let someone rewrite your worth just because they had an opinion.

Criticism feels heavy because we forget to check the source.
So here's a simple filter you can use today.
Before you carry it, ask one question.
Would you ever go to that person for advice?

The relative who is unhappy with their own life.
The stranger in your comments who knows nothing about you.
The coworker who has never built anything.
You wouldn't ask them how to live.

You already do this with money.
You don't take investment tips from someone who's broke.
Use the same rule with your confidence.


So don't let them tell you how you're living.
That's not feedback. That's noise wearing a serious voice.

Keep the criticism that comes with care and a track record.
Drop the rest before you get home.
Not every voice gets a seat at your table.
Some people are mentors. Some are just loud.


Write down three names you actually trust.
Those are your judges.
Everyone else is just watching.
