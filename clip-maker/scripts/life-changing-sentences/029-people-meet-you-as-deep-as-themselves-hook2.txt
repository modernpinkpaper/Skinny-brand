There's a theory that says people can only meet you
as deeply as they've met themselves.

That's why being misunderstood hurts so much.
You think it's a verdict on who you are.
You think if you explained it better, they'd finally see you.
So you keep explaining yourself.

So you shrink the story down to fit them.
You repeat yourself in three different ways.
You apologize for feeling things deeply.
You start calling yourself dramatic.
You go quiet when they call you too much.


But they weren't rejecting you.
They were showing you how far they've gone inside themselves.
That's not your depth. That's their ceiling.
It's not that you're too much. They're just not that deep.

Nobody knows you. They know their version of you.
People don't see the world as it is. They see it as they are.
Some people are windows. Some are walls.
You can't hand someone eyes they never opened.


So stop over-explaining your depth to shallow water.
The right people won't need a translation.
