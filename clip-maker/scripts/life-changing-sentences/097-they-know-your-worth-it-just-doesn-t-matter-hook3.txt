The saddest part is they already know your worth.
They knew it the whole time.
It just didn't change anything.
Being misunderstood would have been easier.
Being seen clearly and left is heavier.

You keep thinking if you explain it better, they'll see you.
So you soften your voice.
You shrink the hurt to fit one text.
You wait for a reply that never comes.

You show up healed, doing better, finally steady.
You post the thing you hoped they'd notice.
You rehearse what you'd say if they came back.


But they weren't confused. They were choosing.
That's not a misunderstanding. That's an answer.
Silence is an answer too.

You don't have to audition for a seat someone gave away.
If they wanted to, they would.
Not because you weren't enough.
Because they weren't.

Your worth was never the question.
Some people are blind.
Some people just look away.
Stop proving. Start protecting.


They left you on the ground.
They don't get to meet you standing.
Let them keep the version they threw away.
