Just because a shirt doesn't fit you doesn't mean your body is wrong.

A number on a tag is not a grade.
It's a guess.
A factory guessed at a shape, picked a size, and called it standard.
It was never measured for you.

Still, you stand in that changing room and blame yourself.
You keep jeans that haven't fit in four years.
You skip the photo, the beach, the dinner, until the zipper agrees.
You tell yourself you'll start living one size from now.


But clothes were made to fit people.
People were not made to fit clothes.
That's not vanity. That's a tape measure with too much power.

Buy the size that lets you breathe.
Cut the tag out if it stings.
Some clothes are simply wrong for you. That's all it means.
Nothing is wrong with the body carrying you through your whole life.


Wear the thing today.
The mirror isn't a judge. It's just glass.
