How can you heal in the same room that made you sick?

That's the question you keep walking away from.
Not because you don't know the answer.
But because leaving means admitting something.
That you walked through the wrong door.

So you stay in the job that hollowed you out.
You stay in the city that never felt like yours.
You stay with someone who stopped choosing you years ago.
And you call it loyalty.
You call it being realistic.


But walking through the wrong door was never the mistake.
Standing in the wrong room for years is.

Those years aren't wasted. They're the proof.
The proof that you tried.
If nothing changes, nothing changes.
If you're not changing it, you're choosing it.

Let go, or be dragged.
Saying it out loud costs you one hard day.
Pretending costs you years.
You can't get better where you got sick.
Some rooms are homes. Some are just rooms you got used to.


Turn around. Walk back out.
The door you came through still opens.
