There's an old chess saying that goes like this.
At the end of the game,
the king and the pawn go in the same box.

Same box. Same silence. Same size.
Nobody gets a bigger one for winning.
And still, you spend your days measuring.
You keep score in a game nobody wins.

You check if your car is nicer.
You argue to be right instead of kind.
You hold a grudge over one sentence.
You skip dinner with people who love you.
Just to impress people who don't.


But everyone you compete with ends where you do.
That's not a race. That's a walk.
We're all just walking each other home.
The people beside you are the point.

The title never leaves the board.
The money never leaves the room.
Everything you own owns a piece of you.

Nobody is special. Everybody is rare.
Some people collect status. Some collect moments.
Only one of those comes with you.


So play the game. Just remember the box.
And be gentle with the other pieces.
