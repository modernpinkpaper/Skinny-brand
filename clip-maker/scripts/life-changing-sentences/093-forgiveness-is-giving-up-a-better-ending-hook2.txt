There's a theory that says forgiveness starts
when you stop asking for a better ending.

Not a better person.
Not an apology.
A better ending.

Because that's what you're really holding onto.
A version where they chose you.
Where they came back and explained everything.

You still rehearse it.
You imagine the message that never came.
You replay the last night and fix your lines.
You wait for the part that makes sense.


But that ending isn't coming.
And waiting for it keeps you inside the hurt.
Forgiveness isn't saying it didn't hurt.
It's saying it hurt, and you're done paying for it.

A grudge feels like power.
It's just weight.
It's not strength. It's a room you never left.
Some stories get a real ending.
Some just get an honest one.

You can forgive the mistake
and still keep the door closed.
You can let it go
without ever calling it okay.


Stop editing a story that already closed.
Give yourself the ending they never will.
