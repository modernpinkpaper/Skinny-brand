There's a theory that says slowly is the fastest way to get anywhere.

Because water doesn't cut the mountain by being strong.
It cuts it by showing up.
Same place. Same drop. Every single day.

But slow progress feels like no progress.
That's why you quit.
Not because you failed.
Because nothing looked different yet.

You read four pages and felt silly.
You went to the gym twice and saw the same body.
You saved a little and the number barely moved.


But three steps forward and two steps back is still one step forward.
That's not failure. That's the shape of it.
Sometimes you get pulled back like a slingshot, right before you're launched.

Change comes in tiny, boring pieces.
Little by little, a little becomes a lot.
You're not stuck. You're just taking your time.
One step back is part of the dance too.
The top of one mountain is the bottom of the next.


So keep dripping on the stone. It's already cracking where you can't see.
