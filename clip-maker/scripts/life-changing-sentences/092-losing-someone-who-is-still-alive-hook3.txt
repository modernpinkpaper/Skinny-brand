The saddest part is when they're still alive.

You don't get a funeral.
You don't get flowers.
Nobody says sorry for your loss.
Because nothing died that anyone can see.

They still walk around wearing the same face.
Same laugh. Same hands.
Same name saved in your phone.
But the person inside it left a long time ago.

You see them in a photo and ache.
You hear their name and go quiet.
You catch yourself talking to them in your head, at 2 a.m., about nothing.


But grief needs an ending, and this one never gives you one.
That's not closure. That's a door left open in the wind.

They didn't hate you. They just stopped choosing you.
And indifference hurts worse than hate ever could.
Some people leave. Some people just stop arriving.
You were loyal to a memory, not a person.


So stop waiting at a grave with no body in it.
Let them be a chapter, and go be the rest of the book.
