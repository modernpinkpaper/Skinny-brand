Is it really winning if you get the point and lose the person?
You can be right.
You can have the facts, the receipts, the better argument.
And still get nothing you actually wanted.

You prove it in the group chat.
You correct your partner mid sentence.
You send the long message that ends the conversation forever.
Being right feels like being good.
But it rarely changes anyone's mind.


So before you reply, ask what you want to happen next.
If you want to be seen as smart, argue.
If you want to be heard, ask a question first.
Let them finish.
Repeat their point back before you answer yours.
Then say the one sentence that matters, and stop talking.

Some people are committed to misunderstanding you.
Nothing you say will fix that.
Save your energy for the ones who are trying.
That's not losing. That's choosing.
Being right ends the talk.
Being effective keeps the door open.


Choose the door.
