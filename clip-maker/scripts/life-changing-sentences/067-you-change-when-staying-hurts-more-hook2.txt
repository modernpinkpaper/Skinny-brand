There's a theory that says you never change until staying hurts more than leaving.
Not when you're inspired.
Not when you finally feel ready.
Only when the ache of the same day crosses a line inside you.

So you wait for motivation that never comes.
You stay in the job that quietly shrinks you.
You keep the friend who makes you smaller.
You say next month, next year, when things calm down.

And the worst part is that it's survivable.
It doesn't hurt enough to force your hand.
It just hurts enough to steal your years.


But you already know what needs to move.
That's not patience. That's a slow goodbye to your own life.
A rut is just a grave with the ends kicked out.

Nothing changes if nothing changes.
Old keys don't open new doors.
Ready isn't a feeling. It's a decision you make while still afraid.
Some pain is a warning. Some pain is a door.


You don't need to feel ready. You just need to stop waiting for it.
