Is it really a connection if you're the only one holding it up?

You've been watering something that stopped growing a long time ago.
You call it patience.
You call it loving hard.

You send the first message every morning.
You plan every visit, every call, every small surprise.
You remember every date they mention. They forget yours.
You apologize for asking for so little.
You check your phone, it stays quiet, and you tell yourself they're just busy.


But you already know.
Part of you has known for months.
That's not busy. That's a choice they keep making.

Stop watering it and you'll see what was ever alive.
Some people are gardens. Some are dry soil you kept believing in.
You are not too much. You were pouring into the wrong place.
Not an option. Not a maybe. Not a backup plan.

The silence won't break you. It will only tell you the truth.


So put the water down, and go where something grows back.
