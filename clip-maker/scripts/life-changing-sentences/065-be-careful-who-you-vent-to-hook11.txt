Always trust the person who never repeats what someone else told them.
Being listened to is not the same as being safe.
Some people nod while they collect.
They are not holding your story. They are storing it.
You were hurting, so you talked. That's human.

Notice who brings you other people's secrets.
Notice who says, don't tell anyone, then tells you anyway.
Notice who asks questions that are a little too specific.
Notice how they talk about the last person who trusted them.


Whoever gossips to you will gossip about you.
That's not friendship. That's material.

So test them first. Give them something small.
Wait a week.
See if it walks into another room.

If it stays quiet, give a little more.
Vent about the feeling, not the details.
Keep the names out of it.
And remember, you are allowed to have a thought you never say out loud.


Silence is not dishonesty. It's a door with a lock.
Choose carefully who gets a key.
