You can't keep every door open just because you're able to.

Every door you hold open
costs you a hand.
So you stand in the hallway,
holding all of them,
walking through none.

You keep the side project alive
but never finish it.
You keep three cities in mind
so you never unpack.
You keep every option saved
and every choice untouched.

Here's the practical part.
Write down everything you want.
Then circle one for this year.
The rest don't disappear.
They just wait their turn.

Then name the price out loud.
Every yes costs money, time,
or someone's disappointment.
Pay it on purpose,
not by accident.

And look at what you own.
Every thing you keep
asks you for space,
money, and attention.
If it doesn't earn that, let it go.


Keeping everything open isn't freedom.
That's a waiting room.
You can have anything you want.
You just can't have all of it at once.


So choose one door this week,
walk through it,
and let the hallway close behind you.
