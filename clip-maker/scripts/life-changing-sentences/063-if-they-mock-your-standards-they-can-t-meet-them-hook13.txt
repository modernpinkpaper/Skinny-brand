Just because someone calls your standards too high
doesn't mean they are.
It usually means they can't reach them.
So they make you feel wrong for asking.

That's how it starts.
You want good morning texts, so you ask for less.
You want honesty, so you settle for explanations.
You want to be chosen, so you settle for being remembered.

You call it being easygoing.
You call it not being difficult.
But every time you lower the bar,
they learn how little they can give you.


But what you keep allowing becomes the rule.
That's not compromise. That's teaching them.
You can't be half loved and call it enough.

Guilt is not love.
Someone who wanted you wouldn't make you ask twice.
Don't make someone your priority while you're their option.
Some people are effort. Some people are excuses.
And the person you choose shows the love you think you deserve.


Raise the bar back up.
The right person won't complain about the height.
