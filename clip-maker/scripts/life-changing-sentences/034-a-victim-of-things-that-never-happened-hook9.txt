All of us are survivors of disasters that never happened.

You've buried people who are still alive.
You've lost jobs you still have.
You've read the bad news before anyone wrote it.

Your brain calls this preparing.
It isn't.
It's paying a bill that never comes.

You lie awake rehearsing the phone call.
You practice the argument in the shower.
You read the message twice, hunting for a tone that isn't there.
And in the morning, nothing happened.
Nothing except you, tired.


But here's the part nobody tells you.
Worry never protected you from anything.
It only made you live it twice.
Once in your head, and once if it ever came.

Fear doesn't know the truth. It only knows what it's scared of.
That's not planning. That's suffering on schedule.
And if it wasn't this, your mind would just find something else.
The real thing, when it comes, never looks like the rehearsal.


Worry doesn't take tomorrow's trouble. It takes today's peace.
So give today back to yourself.
