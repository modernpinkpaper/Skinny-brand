There's a theory that says you get two lives.
And the second one starts the day you realize you only ever had one.

Most people never reach it.
You're not living yet.
You're rehearsing.
Waiting for a better year, a calmer month, a braver version of you.

The trip you'll take when work slows down.
The call you'll make once you know what to say.
The thing you've wanted since you were nine, still waiting for permission.


But the time passes anyway.
Five years go by whether you start or not.
It's not that you're lazy.
You just thought you had more room.

Forever is only made of nows.
This one counts.
So does the next.
Getting older isn't the punishment.
It's the part some people never got.

You don't need a near-death moment to wake up.
You just need to stop calling this the warm-up.


So do the small brave thing today.
Not when you're ready.
Ready is not coming.
Your second life starts the second you stop waiting.
