You can't wait for certainty just because the wrong choice scares you.

Waiting feels safe.
But waiting is also a choice.
You're choosing the same year again.

You've been deciding about that job for two years.
You've been thinking about that move since last spring.
You've been rewriting the same message in your head all week.
And every month you don't choose, life chooses for you.


Here's the practical part.
There is no perfect path waiting to be found.
A decision becomes right because you work it.
Nothing gets clearer while you stand still.

So give yourself a deadline. Three days, not three years.
Write down the worst case, then write down how you'd survive it.
Pick the option with more information at the end of it.
Then commit for ninety days before you judge it.

Most choices are doors, not tattoos.
You can walk back through. You can change your mind.
Just don't stand in the hallway forever.


Choose, then make it right.
