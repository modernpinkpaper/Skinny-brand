Is it really safe if it hurts a little every single day?

Avoidance feels like protection.
It feels like you bought yourself time.
But you didn't cancel the pain.
You just spread it thin across every hour.

The message you keep not sending.
The call you keep pushing to tomorrow.
The room you leave when a certain name comes up.
The door you walk past for weeks.

Each one costs you a little.
And you pay it again the next morning.


But here's what you already know.
Fear is a mile wide and an inch deep.
It looks like an ocean until you step in.
Then you find it barely covers your feet.

The waiting is the worst part.
The thing itself takes ten minutes.
It's not too hard. It's just unfamiliar.
It's not that you can't. It's that you never have.

Some fears are walls. Most are fog.
A ship is safest in the harbor.
That is not what ships are for.


So do the scary thing badly.
And let yourself put the weight down.
