The reason you're so good at accepting crumbs
is because you quietly decided that's your price.

Nobody handed you that number.
You set it. Alone. Years ago.
And every person since has just agreed with you.

You call them the bare minimum, but you stay.
You explain their behavior to your friends.
You shrink the story so it sounds normal.
You wait days for a reply and call it patience.
You thank them for the smallest kindness.


But you're not unlucky in love.
You're consistent. That's not the same thing.
This isn't bad luck. It's a mirror.

The person beside you is a report card.
Not on them. On what you think you're worth.
You teach people how to hold you.

Nobody can love you the way you want to be loved
until you show them it's possible.
That part is your job.
Not their job. Yours.

Some people are chapters. Some are proof.
Proof of the number you set.


So raise it. Quietly. Today.
And let the next person meet you there.
