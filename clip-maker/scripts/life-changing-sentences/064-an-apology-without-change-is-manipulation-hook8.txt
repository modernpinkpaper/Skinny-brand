Some people don't apologize to fix it.
They apologize to keep it.

This is hard because the words sound right.
They say your name softly.
They look tired, and sorry, and sincere.
And you want that to be enough.

So you tell them once. They hear you.
You tell them twice. They nod.
You tell them a third time.
And you start to wonder if you're the problem.

You watch the same night happen again.
Different month, same hurt, new words.
And nothing changes but the date.
You keep hoping the next sorry is the real one.


But you were never asking for much.
You were asking for proof.
Hope is not evidence.
An apology is not a plan.
It's a pause.
That's not remorse. That's maintenance.

Change is quiet.
It doesn't announce itself.
It just stops happening to you.

Some people are sorry.
Some are just sorry you noticed.
What you allow, you agree to.


Stop counting the apologies.
Start counting the days it didn't happen again.
