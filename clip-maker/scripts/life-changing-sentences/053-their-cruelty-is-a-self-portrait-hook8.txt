Cruel people don't describe you. They describe themselves.

Psychology calls it projection.
When someone can't hold a feeling about themselves,
they hand it to you and call it your name.

So listen to what they accuse you of.
Too loud. Too selfish. Too much.
Those are usually the words they fear most about themselves.

You can test it.
Watch how they speak about everyone else.
Watch how they act when nobody is impressed.
Watch who they are when there's nothing to win.


But here's the part that keeps you stuck.
When someone attacks you, you go searching your own character for the flaw.
That's not honesty. That's absorbing.

Confidence doesn't need an audience. Insecurity does.
A person at peace has no reason to make you smaller.
Their words were a self portrait, not a mirror.

So next time it lands, ask one question.
Is this feedback, or is this a confession?
Feedback comes with care. Confessions come with contempt.


Keep the lesson. Give the shame back to its owner.
