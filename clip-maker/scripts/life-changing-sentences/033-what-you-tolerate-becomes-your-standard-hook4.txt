You can't stay quiet and expect people to guess your limits.

Silence sounds like permission.
Every time you let something slide, you write a rule.
And people follow the rules you give them.

The joke about you that goes a little too far.
The plan they cancel an hour before, again.
The message you answer in seconds while yours sits for days.


But this is fixable.
It's not a fight. It's a sentence.

Say it the first time, not the tenth.
Name the behavior, not the person.
Don't talk to me like that. Then stop explaining.
Explaining turns your boundary into a debate.

Give them one chance to correct it.
If they repeat it, give them less of you.
Less time. Less access. Less of your story.
Do it calmly. Do it every time.

You don't need a speech.
You don't need them to agree.
You just need to stop rewarding it.
People adjust to what costs them something.


Your standard is whatever you let happen twice.
So decide today what you stop allowing.
