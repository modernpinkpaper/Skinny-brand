Always trust the fed version of a person.

Hunger doesn't just live in your stomach.
It moves into your voice.
It shortens your patience.
It makes small things feel like betrayal.

You've felt it.
The fight that started over dishes at nine at night.
The text you sent before lunch that you read back later and didn't recognize.
The person you love, suddenly sounding like an enemy, over nothing.


But that wasn't your truth talking.
That was your body, writing with your mouth.
It's not honesty. It's low blood sugar and a long day.
Nobody wins an argument on an empty stomach.
The same goes for tired, scared and stressed.

So make a rule.
No hard talks hungry. No hard talks tired.
Eat first. Drink water. Sit down.
Then say the thing.

If it still matters after food, it's real.
If it disappears after a sandwich, it was never the issue.
Impatience isn't insight.
It's just selfishness with a clock attached.


Feed the body before you question the heart.
