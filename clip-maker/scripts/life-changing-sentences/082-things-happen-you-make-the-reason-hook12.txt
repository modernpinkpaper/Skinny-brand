Is it really fate
if you're the one who made sense of it?

Because when something breaks you,
people hand you the same sentence.
Everything happens for a reason.
And it hurts twice.
Once when it happened.
Once when they said that.

You lost the job and nobody explained why.
You sat in a hospital hallway waiting.
You loved someone who left without a reason.


But here's what nobody says out loud.
Things happen. You make the reason.
That's not denial. That's power.
It's happening for you, not to you.

The pain didn't arrive with a purpose.
You gave it one.
You turned your worst year into something useful.
You learned the thing that saves a stranger later.

Suffering is only pointless if you leave it empty.
Three steps forward, two steps back.
That's still movement.

Some meaning is handed to you.
Some meaning is built.
Yours was built.
Life isn't about finding yourself.
It's about making yourself.


So stop waiting for life to explain.
Write the reason yourself.
Then keep walking.
