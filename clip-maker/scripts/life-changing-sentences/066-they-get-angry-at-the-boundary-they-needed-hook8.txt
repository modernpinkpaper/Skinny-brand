People who respect you don't get angry at your boundary.
They adjust.

This is hard because anger feels like evidence.
Someone raises their voice and you assume you were cruel.
So you take the limit back.
You apologize for having needs.
You were taught that a good person never disappoints anyone.

You said no to a favor, and they went quiet for days.
You asked for one evening alone, and suddenly you're selfish.
You stopped fixing their mess, and they called you cold.


But look at who got angry.
It wasn't everyone.
It was the people who needed you to have no limits.
That's not you being harsh.
That's the boundary doing its job.

Their anger is information, not a verdict.
Your yes means nothing if your no isn't real.
Their mess was never your responsibility.
Some people love you.
Some people love how useful you are.


You are allowed to be loved without being used.
Protect your peace the way you would protect a child.
Without apology.
