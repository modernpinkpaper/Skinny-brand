How can anyone love you when you never let them meet you?

You show the easy version.
The calm one. The funny one.
The one who never needs anything.
And people love that version.
So you keep that version alive.

You laugh off the thing that hurt you.
You say you're fine before they ask.
You delete the long message and send the short one.


But the love that reaches a mask never reaches you.
That's not being loved. That's being approved of.

Being known is embarrassing.
Someone sees your fear, your mess, your 2 a.m. thoughts.
And stays anyway.
That's the only love that feeds you.

You can't be loved and hidden at the same time.
Pick one.

And if you only accept the love you think you deserve,
then start by treating yourself like someone worth knowing.
Speak to yourself the way you'd speak to a friend.


Let one person see the unedited version this week.
Not the highlight. The whole thing.
