Is it really the worst life if you'd still take it back?

Imagine everyone threw their problems into one pile.
Every person you envy. Every stranger. Everyone.
And you got to choose again.

You'd walk around that pile slowly.
You'd read what other people carry quietly.
And you'd pick up your own problems and go home.

Because you know yours. You've survived yours.
Someone out there is hiding a pain
nobody would even let them say out loud.
Your struggles get sympathy. Not everyone's do.

You compare yourself upward all day.
The nicer house. The easier childhood.
The person who seems to never try.
But you never look sideways.


That's not perspective. That's punishment.
You're measuring your whole life
against other people's best five minutes.

Your life isn't cursed. It's just yours.
Some problems are proof you got something worth protecting.
And remember, you once prayed for the things you walk past now.


So notice one of them today. Out loud.
