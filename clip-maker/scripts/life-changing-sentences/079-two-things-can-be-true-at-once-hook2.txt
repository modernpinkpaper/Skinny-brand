There's a theory that says your brain wants one verdict, not two.

So you take everything you feel and you vote.
One feeling wins. The other gets called fake.
And then you spend weeks arguing with yourself.

You miss who you used to be, and you like who you are now.
You wanted them to stay, and you were right to go.
You love your family, and you needed distance from them.
You hear the news and feel free and disappointed in the same breath.


But a feeling is not a courtroom.
You don't need a winner.
It's not a verdict. It's information.

So try this. Swap the word but for and.
Not grateful but tired. Grateful and tired.
Then stop asking which feeling is the real one.
Ask what each one is trying to tell you.

Two truths don't cancel each other. They explain each other.
Some feelings need answers. Some just need room.


So stop picking a side against yourself.
