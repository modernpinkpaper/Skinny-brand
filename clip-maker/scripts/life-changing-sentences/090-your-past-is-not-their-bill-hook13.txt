Just because someone hurt you doesn't mean the next person will.

But that's not how it feels.
Once you've known betrayal, you start seeing it everywhere.
You call it intuition.
Sometimes it's just fear with a better name.

So you test them.
You go quiet to see if they chase you.
You read one slow reply like a confession.
You ask the same question three ways, hoping they slip.


But they never did anything.
You're handing them a bill for a debt someone else ran up.
That's not protection. That's a trial with no crime.

Your past is not their sentence to serve.
Not every silence is a secret.
Not every kind person is a trap.
Some people are lessons.
Some are proof you were wrong to stop hoping.

You don't owe anyone your softness twice.
But you owe yourself the chance to use it again.


So let the new person be new.
Watch who they are, not who they remind you of.
