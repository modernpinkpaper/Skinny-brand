You can't call it kindness just because you felt it.
Most good things die unsaid.
You notice something true. You mean it. You say nothing.
The moment passes, and you carry it forever.

You thought your friend looked lighter again and never told her.
You watched someone handle a hard day well and stayed quiet.
You read a message twice, smiled, and closed the app.


But a kind thought does nothing.
A kind sentence does everything.
It's not awkward. It's just unpracticed.

So say it the same day you think it.
Be specific, and name the exact thing you noticed.
Keep it short. Don't wait for a reason or a birthday.
Send it badly rather than never.
Tell people who they are becoming, not only what they did.

The smallest kind word beats the grandest intention.
People don't care what you know until they feel you care.
Every nice thought you swallow is a gift nobody ever got.


Someone is one sentence away from a better week.
Go say it.
