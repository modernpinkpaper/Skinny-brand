You can't call it a no just because you never asked.

Nobody rejected you.
You did it for them.
You played the conversation in your head,
heard the no, and walked away quiet.

The raise you never brought up.
The discount you assumed they'd refuse.
The help you needed, and carried alone.
The person who never knew you liked them.


So here's what to do instead.
Ask plainly. One sentence.
Say what you want, and when you want it.
No apology in front of it.
No long story behind it.

Write it down first if your voice shakes.
Send it before you talk yourself out of it.
Then let them answer.
A no costs you nothing you had.
A yes changes your whole week.

Silence isn't protection. It's a guaranteed no.
Rejection has a limit. Regret doesn't.
You're grown. You're allowed to want things out loud.

Ask ten times, get told no eight.
You're still up two.


So ask. Let them be the one to say no.
