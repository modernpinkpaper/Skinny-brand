There's a psychology rule called make the unconscious conscious.
It says whatever you don't look at still runs you.
It picks your jobs. It picks your fights.
And when it works, you call it fate.

You quit the third job with the same kind of boss.
You meet someone new and have an old argument.
A small comment lands like a punch you've felt before.


But that's not bad luck. That's a pattern.
And a pattern is something you can learn.

Here's the test.
When the feeling is bigger than the moment,
it isn't the moment.
If you're hysterical, it's historical.

Write down what happened and what you felt.
Then write down how old you felt.
Do that five times and the shape shows up.
Then name it out loud before it moves you.

Noticing isn't a cure. It's a crack of light.
Once you see the habit, its days are numbered.
The life you're living now is the life you built.


So put down the old bricks.
Or you'll keep building the same house.
