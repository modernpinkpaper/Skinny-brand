There's a theory that says your brain can't tell reward from rescue.

If you celebrate with a drink,
and then cope with the same drink,
your body stops knowing what it means.
The signal gets blurry.
Joy and escape start using the same door.

You order the same food after a promotion
and after a fight.
You shop when you're proud
and when you're empty.
You scroll to reward yourself
and to disappear.


But here's the useful part.
A binge isn't weakness.
It's motivation with bad aim.
Your body is asking for something.
It just learned the wrong address.

So split them.
Pick one thing that is only for celebrating.
Pick different things for the hard nights.
A walk. A shower. A call.
Something that leaves you calmer, not further away.

Name it out loud before you reach.
Am I rewarding myself, or rescuing myself?
That one question does most of the work.


Reward should feel like a gift.
Not like medicine you can't stop taking.
