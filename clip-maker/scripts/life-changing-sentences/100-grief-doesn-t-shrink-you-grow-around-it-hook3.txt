The saddest part is nobody tells you grief doesn't end.

You keep waiting for the day you wake up finished.
You mark the months.
You count the years.
And when it comes back, you think you did it wrong.

A song in the grocery store.
Their handwriting on an old card.
Someone laughing the exact same way in a crowd.
Your own voice saying we when you mean I.


But grief was never a wound that closes.
It's the price you pay for loving someone that much.
It's not a sign you failed.
It's a sign it was real.

You don't get smaller around it. You grow.
New mornings. New people. New reasons to stay.
The ache stays the same size.
Your life gets bigger.

Some days it's a whisper.
Some days it's the whole room.
Tears aren't weakness. They're grief leaving your body.
Anger is just grief in a heavier coat.


So stop waiting to be done.
Keep building a life wide enough to carry them with you.
