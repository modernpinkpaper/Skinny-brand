The reason you're so good at quitting new things
is because someone once called you gifted.

You learned it early.
Smart kids don't struggle.
Smart kids get it the first time.
So if it felt hard, you must not be smart.

Now you're grown.
You only pick up what you already know how to hold.
You quit the guitar in week two.
You delete the drawing before anyone sees it.
You read about the class instead of signing up.


But that's not laziness.
That's fear wearing a nice outfit.
Perfectionism is just fear that learned good manners.

Being bad at something is not proof you're broken.
It's proof you're finally learning again.
Every skill you admire started as someone's ugly first try.
The price of learning anything new is being a beginner forever.


So go be messy today.
Ask yourself tonight how you failed.
If nothing went wrong, you didn't grow.
You don't have to be perfect anymore.
You're allowed to just be good.
