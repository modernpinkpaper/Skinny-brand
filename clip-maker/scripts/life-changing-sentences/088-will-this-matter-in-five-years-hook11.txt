Always trust the five year test.

Before you spend another hour angry,
ask one question.
Will this still matter in five years?
If the answer is no,
it doesn't get five more minutes.

The rude comment from a stranger.
The text someone left on read.
The mistake you replayed at 2 a.m.
Five years from now, you won't remember any of it.


But some things do pass the test.
Your health. Your money. The people you love.
Those get your worry, your time, your full attention.

So sort it fast.
Name the thing out loud.
Give it a year number.
Write it down if you have to.
Five years, act on it. Five days, let it go.

Most of what drains you isn't a problem.
It's noise wearing a problem's clothes.

Almost nothing people do is about you.
Your peace costs more than being right.
And the people who mind, don't matter.
The people who matter, won't mind.


Spend your worry like money. You only get so much.
