There's a theory called the burnt toast theory.

It says the small delays in your day are protection.
The toast that burned. The keys you couldn't find.
The five minutes that made you late for everything.

You sat in traffic and gripped the wheel.
You missed the train by ten seconds.
You stood in the slowest line in the store.
And you called it a bad morning.


But you never get to see the other version.
The one where you left on time.
The one where nothing stopped you at all.
That's not a bad morning. That's a door closing quietly.

Not everything that slows you down is working against you.
Just because it could have been different doesn't mean it would have been better.
Sometimes the delay is the detour that saves you.
Sometimes the obstacle is the path.
Some minutes cost you. Some minutes save you.
You never get to know which one it was.


So burn the toast. Sit in the traffic.
Trust the timing you didn't choose.
