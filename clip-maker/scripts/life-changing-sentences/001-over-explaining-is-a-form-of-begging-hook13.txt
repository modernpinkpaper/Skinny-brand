Just because someone hears you doesn't mean they ever wanted to understand you.

And that's why you keep talking.
You add one more reason.
One more example.
One more sorry you don't owe.
Because somewhere back there, you weren't believed the first time.

You explain why you're late, twice.
You explain why you're tired, with proof.
You explain a simple no like you're defending a case in court.

Then you walk away wondering why you feel so small.


That's not communication. That's begging.
Not begging for love. Begging for permission to exist as you are.

Some people are confused. Some people are committed.
The confused ask one question and get it.
The committed will misread you in any language you choose.

You were never hard to understand.
You were just talking to someone who decided not to.


So say the true thing once. Clearly. Kindly.
Then let the silence do the rest.
Silence can't be twisted.
And the right people won't need the long version.
