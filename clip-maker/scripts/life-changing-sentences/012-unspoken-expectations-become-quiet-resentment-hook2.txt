There's a theory that says unspoken expectations are just resentment on a timer.

You build a quiet test in your head.
They never get to see the questions.
Then you grade them.

You wait for someone to offer help while you carry everything.
You hope they text first on your worst day.
You go quiet and wait for someone to notice.


But nobody in your life is a mind reader.
It's not that they don't care.
It's that they were never told.

So do it in one plain sentence.
Name the thing, the day, and the help you want.
Say, can you call me tonight.
Ask the person once, clearly, while you're calm.
Then let their answer be real information.

A yes gives you what you needed.
A no shows you where to place them.
Either way you stop guessing.

Rejection rarely leaves you worse than silence did.
Closed mouths stay hungry.
The answer is no until you ask.


Say what you need out loud, while you still like them.
