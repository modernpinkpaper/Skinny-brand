There's a theory that says
the version of you who survives
the worst day of your life
isn't born yet.

That version appears in the moment it happens.
So when you sit here tonight,
trying to solve it already,
you're using today's strength
for a day that hasn't come.

That's why it always feels impossible.
You lie awake planning a call nobody made.
You imagine the words. The empty room.
You rehearse a grief that isn't yours yet.

And when you fall apart in your head,
you call it proof you couldn't cope.


But it isn't proof.
It's a test with no answers.
You already survived things you never saw coming.
You didn't plan your way through them.
You met them.

Worrying only makes you suffer twice.
If you lived through the event,
you can live through the memory.
It's not that you don't have the strength.
It's that it hasn't arrived yet.


So stop asking today's you
to carry tomorrow's weight.
That person will show up.
Trust them.
