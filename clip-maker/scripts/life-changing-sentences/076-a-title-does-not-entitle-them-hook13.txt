Just because someone has a title in your life
doesn't mean they're entitled to stay in it.

Mother. Brother. Oldest friend.
Those are words, not permission slips.
You were taught the word came with a door key.

You keep answering the phone because of who they are.
You explain yourself to someone who never listens.
You apologize for the mood they put you in.
You sit at a table where you get smaller every year.


But you can accept people exactly as they are
and still stop handing them your whole life.
That's not cutting them off. That's placing them where they belong.

You either accept who someone is,
or you accept how they treat you.
Guilt is not the same as loyalty.
You can't always change the people in your life.
You can change how close they get.

Some people are family. Some are just familiar.
Love can stay. Access can shrink.


So keep the title if you need to.
Just stop letting it choose your seat.
