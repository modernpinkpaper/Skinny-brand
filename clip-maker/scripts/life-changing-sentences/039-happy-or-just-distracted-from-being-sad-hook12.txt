Is it really happiness if it stops the second the noise stops?

You're not lying to anyone.
You're just very good at staying busy.
Because busy feels better than quiet.

You fill every gap.
You scroll until your eyes hurt.
You keep something playing in every room.
You say yes to plans you don't even want.
You fall asleep to a screen so nothing can catch up to you.

Then the phone dies.
The room goes silent.
And it's all still there.


But distraction was never healing.
It's not peace. It's a pause.
And a pause always ends.

You don't drown from falling in.
You drown from staying under.
What you avoid runs you.
What you face lets you go.

Sadness isn't asking you to live in it.
It's asking you to look at it once.
Ten quiet minutes.
No screen. No noise.
Just you, telling the truth.


You can't be comfortably miserable forever.
So sit with it tonight, before it decides to sit with you.
