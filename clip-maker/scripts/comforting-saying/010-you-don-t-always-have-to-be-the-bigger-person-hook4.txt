You can't keep shrinking just because you're the only one willing to bend.
Somewhere along the way you learned that being good meant being quiet.
That keeping the peace was your job alone.
So you swallowed it. Every single time.
And everyone around you got comfortable.

You apologize first, even when you weren't wrong.
You say yes with a tired voice and a tight chest.
You explain your no for ten minutes so nobody calls you cold.
They call you mature. They call you easy to love.
They never ask what it costs you.


But being the bigger person again and again isn't growth.
It's you doing the growing for people who refuse to.
That's not kindness. That's self-erasure with better manners.
You are allowed to disappoint someone.
No is a full sentence. You can stop there.
Just because you can carry it doesn't mean you should.

You don't have to be everyone's sunshine.
You were never built to be everybody's soft place.
Some people are warmed by you. Some are just used to you.


So put yourself first this time, and don't apologize for the quiet it brings.
