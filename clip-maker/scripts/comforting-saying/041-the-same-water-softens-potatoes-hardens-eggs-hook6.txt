If you go through something hard and come out bitter, that's a reaction.
If you come out softer, that's a reaction too.
But if you notice which one you're becoming while it's happening, that's a choice.

The same boiling water softens a potato and hardens an egg.
The water isn't deciding anything.
What's inside you is.

And what's inside you got built by small things.
Who you talk to after a bad day.
What you tell yourself about why it happened.
Whether you let anyone see you struggling.


But hard is not the same as strong.
A bowling ball looks unbreakable.
Drop it enough times and it chips.
A bouncy ball gives a little, then comes back.

So bend on purpose.
Say the heavy thing out loud to one person.
Name what hurt instead of burying it.
Let yourself rest before you snap.

That's not going soft. That's staying whole.


You don't get to pick the water.
You get to keep choosing what you're made of.
