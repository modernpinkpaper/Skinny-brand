Just because someone doesn't understand you
doesn't mean they don't accept you.

You learned the opposite.
You learned that love has to be earned with an explanation.
So you got good at translating yourself.
Smaller words. Softer edges. Shorter stories.

You say sorry for how excited you get.
You cut the interesting part out of the sentence.
You watch their face to see if you've said too much.
And when they look confused,
you apologize for existing out loud.


But being understood and being accepted
are two different things.
One needs a translator.
The other just needs someone willing to stay.

That's not a flaw in your wiring.
That's a flaw in their patience.

The right people don't ask you to make sense.
They ask you to keep talking.
They say, I love the way your mind works,
and they mean it before they get it.

Some people need a manual.
Some people just need a chair pulled up beside you.


Stop shrinking to fit a language
that was never built for you.
The ones who belong to you will learn yours.
