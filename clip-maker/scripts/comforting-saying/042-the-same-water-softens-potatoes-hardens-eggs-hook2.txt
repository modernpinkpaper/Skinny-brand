There's a theory that says the same boiling water
softens the potato and hardens the egg.
Same heat. Same pot. Same time.
Two completely different results.

So people say it's about what you're made of.
But that's only half the lesson.
A potato doesn't choose. An egg doesn't choose.
You do.

Think about the last hard year you had.
Someone went through the same thing and came out kinder.
Someone else came out sharp and closed off.
And you've been both of those people.


Here's what actually decides it.
It's not the pain. It's what you do inside it.
Softening is not weakness. It's staying open on purpose.
Hardening is not strength. It's a locked door.

So watch the small things.
Do you still ask people how they are.
Do you still say sorry first.
Do you still let something move you.

That's the real test.
Not how much you survived.
How much of you survived.


You can't always pick the water.
You can always pick what it makes you.
