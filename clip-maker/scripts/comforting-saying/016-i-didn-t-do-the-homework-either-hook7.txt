Comfort is weird.

It almost never comes from good advice.
Nobody ever fixed you with a speech.
What actually helped was smaller than that.

Someone leaning over before class and saying they didn't do the homework either.
Someone admitting they haven't started the assignment.
Someone saying they're behind too, and meaning it.

And something in your chest finally loosened.
Not because the problem went away.
But because you stopped being the only one.


Because you were never scared of failing.
You were scared of failing alone.
That's not weakness. That's being human.

You don't need someone to carry your weight.
You just need to know someone else is carrying theirs.
Two cups, both uneven, sitting side by side.

Advice makes you feel small.
Company makes you feel real.
Some people fix things. Some people just sit down next to you.
The second kind saves more lives.


So the next time you're behind, say it out loud.
Someone near you is waiting to hear they're not alone.
