Always trust the friend who checks if your bowl is full.

Most people only look to compare.
They measure your plate against theirs.
They count. They keep score.
And somewhere, you learned to do it too.

You scroll and feel behind.
You hear about someone's new job and shrink.
You do quiet math at family dinners.
You win something small and still feel poor.

You tell yourself you just want to do well.
But you don't want enough.
You want more than them.
And more than them keeps moving.


Enough is not a spot on a scoreboard.
It's not a spot on a stage.
Enough is quiet. Enough is a feeling.

Not everyone is meant to be watched.
Someone has to sit down and clap.
That's not a smaller life. That's a full one.

If your days make you happy, you're winning.
Nobody gets to grade that but you.


So stop counting what they have.
Start asking if they have enough.
That's the day comparison lets you go.
