The saddest part is how good you've gotten at saying you're fine.
You say it fast. You say it first.
You say it before anyone can ask twice.
And people believe you, because you made it easy for them.

You answer the hard question with a joke.
You hold the door for everyone while your hands are shaking.
You cry in the shower and come out smiling.
Somewhere along the way you learned that strong meant quiet.
So you carry it alone and call that maturity.


But just because it doesn't show doesn't mean you're not bleeding.
That's not strength. That's a performance.
And nobody can hold a person they've never really met.
You don't have to perform well to be loved.

You're not a mess. You're a feeling person in a messy world.
You're allowed to be tired. You're allowed to be angry.
You're allowed to still be sad about it.
Some people want your best. Some people want the truth.


Find the ones who want the truth, and let the mask drop.
