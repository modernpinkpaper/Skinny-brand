There are some people in this world who don't need you to explain yourself.
They just let you be strange.
No follow up questions.
No confused look.

Because most of your life has been translating.
You say a thought, then you explain the thought.
Then you apologize for the thought.
You shrink your words to fit smaller rooms.
You watch their eyes to see if you lost them again.

Then one day someone says they love the way your mind works.
And something in you goes quiet.
That's not a compliment. That's relief.
Being understood feels nice.
Being accepted feels safe.


But you were never confusing.
You were just uncommon.
Nobody is owed a translation of you.
You are not a puzzle someone has to solve before they love you.

Some people need a map of you before they stay.
Some people just stay.
That's not patience. That's love.
And you are allowed to stop saying sorry for being yourself.


So stop editing yourself down into something easy.
The right people will learn your language.
