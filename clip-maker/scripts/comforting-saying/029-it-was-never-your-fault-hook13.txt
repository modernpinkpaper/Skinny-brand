Just because someone blamed you doesn't mean the blame was ever yours.

You were a kid.
You were handed something you didn't build.
And you've been apologizing for it ever since.

You learned to shrink before anyone asked.
You learned to read a room like a warning.
You learned that peace meant staying small.

Maybe it was a house that never felt safe.
Maybe it was someone who called their anger your fault.
Maybe it was a place that used something holy against you.


But the hurt was theirs.
The choice was theirs.
The hands that did it were never yours.
That's not your shame. That's their shadow.

You were not broken. You were reacting.
Normal reactions to things that were never normal.
No child earns that.
No child deserves that.

Some wounds are yours to heal.
Not every wound is yours to own.


So set it down tonight.
It was never your fault.
And it was never yours to carry.
Forgive yourself for surviving the only way you knew.
