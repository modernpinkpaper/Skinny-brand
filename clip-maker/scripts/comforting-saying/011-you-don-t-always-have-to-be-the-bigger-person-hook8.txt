Kind people don't grow out of conflict. They get assigned to swallow it.

Somewhere along the way you learned that being mature means being quiet.
So you became the one who apologizes first.
The one who lets it go.
The one who keeps the peace by keeping the pain.

You say it's fine when it isn't.
You soften your voice so nobody has to hear your hurt.
You explain yourself for the fifth time to someone who never planned to listen.


But being the bigger person over and over isn't growth.
It's just you shrinking politely.
That's not kindness. That's self-erasure with good manners.

Maybe the people around you are small and need to grow.
Maybe your no was never rude. Just unfamiliar to them.
Just because you can carry it doesn't mean you should.

You are not here to be everyone's sunshine.
Some people need a friend. Some people need a doormat.
You get to decide which one you are.


So put yourself first this time, and don't apologize for the quiet it brings.
