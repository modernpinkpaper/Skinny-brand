The happiest people don't fall apart loudly.
They fall apart politely.
You became the warm one.
The safe one.
The one who always says it's fine.

Somewhere it stopped being a mood.
It became a job.
You answer the late messages.
You remember everyone's bad week.
You walk into a room and lift it.
Nobody asks who lifts you.

Someone tells you how happy you always seem.
You smile and say thank you.
Then something in your chest goes quiet.
So you learn to time it.
You cry before you leave the house.
You say a lot without saying anything.


But you are not a mess.
You are a feeling person in a heavy world.
You are allowed to feel this much.
That's not weakness.
That's the cost of caring out loud.

The biggest smiles hide the biggest grief.
You already knew that.
You're not being dramatic.
You're being honest, finally.
Some people are sunshine.
Even sunshine goes down at night.


You don't have to be everyone's good day.
Let someone be yours.
