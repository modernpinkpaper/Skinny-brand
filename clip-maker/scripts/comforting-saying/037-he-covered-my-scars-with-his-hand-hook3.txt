The saddest part is hiding the proof that you survived.

Long sleeves in summer.
The story edited every time someone asks.
A quick laugh so nobody looks too long.
You think the marks spell weak.
You think people see your worst night.
You think they see nothing else.

So you cover them yourself.
You keep that year off the table.
You skip the part where you almost left.
You say you're fine before anyone asks twice.
You apologize for a body that fought for you.
You call the bravest thing you did shameful.


But someone who loves you covers them with a hand.
And says, see, gone, and you're still here.
The point was never that they vanished.
The point is that you didn't.
That's not damage. That's evidence.
Not proof you broke. Proof you held.


Some scars are wounds. Some are receipts.
You don't have to prove you're strong enough.
You already did.
So stop hiding the part of you that stayed.
