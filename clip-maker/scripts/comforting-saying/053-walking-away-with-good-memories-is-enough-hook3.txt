The saddest part is how long you stay
just to prove it wasn't a waste.
You hold on because leaving feels like failing.
Like every good day would turn into a lie.
So you stretch something that already ended.

You reread old messages at midnight.
You keep a seat open for someone who stopped coming.
You defend them to people who watched you cry.
You call it loyalty.


But something ending is not something failing.
A song isn't ruined because it stops playing.
It's not proof you were wrong. It's proof you were there.
You can be grateful and still go.
You can love the memory and close the door.

Not everyone was meant to stay forever.
Some were meant to stay long enough.
That's not a loss. That's a chapter.
And chapters are supposed to end.
They get left behind either way.
Not by your anger. By your life moving on.


Some people are homes. Some are windows you needed for a while.
So take the good with you, and turn the page.
