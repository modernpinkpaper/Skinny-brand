All of us are quietly ranking our pain.

You hear someone else's story and you shrink your own.
You tell yourself it isn't that bad.
You say other people have it worse.
So you swallow it and keep moving.

You cry in the car and call it nothing.
You say I'm fine before anyone even asks.
You scroll past the help you need
because you think you haven't earned it yet.


But pain was never a competition.
Seven feet of water drowns you
just like twenty feet does.
It's not smaller. It's just quieter.

Just because it doesn't show
doesn't mean you're not bleeding.
Your worst day might be someone else's good one.
That doesn't make it less heavy for you.

You're allowed to be sad about it.
You're allowed to be angry about it.
You're allowed to be tired
even when you survived the thing.

Some wounds are loud. Some are silent.
They both still need care.


Stop waiting until it gets bad enough.
It already hurts. That's enough.
