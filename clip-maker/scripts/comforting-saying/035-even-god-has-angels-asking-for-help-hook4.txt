You can't call it strength just because nobody saw you break.

You learned to handle it alone.
You got good at saying you're fine.
And somewhere along the way
you decided that asking for help
would make you a weight.

So you cry in the shower.
You type the message and delete it.
You ask your friend about their day
and never once mention yours.


But needing someone is not being a burden.
That's not strength. That's silence wearing strength's clothes.
Asking isn't weakness. It's trust.

Even the oldest stories
give the strongest one help.
Nobody was built to hold it all.

Your cup can be full.
Theirs can be empty.
You can still sit side by side.

People who love you
don't want you perfect.
They want you honest.

Saying it out loud
takes the weight out of your chest
and puts it in the room.


So tell one person. Just one.
You were never meant to be the only one holding you.
