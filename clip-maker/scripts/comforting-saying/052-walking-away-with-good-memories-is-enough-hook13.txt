Just because something ended doesn't mean it failed.

You keep staying because leaving feels like proof you got it wrong.
So you hold on to something that already finished.
And you call that loyalty.

You reread old messages looking for the part you missed.
You keep a seat open for someone who stopped showing up.
You tell people it's complicated when really it's just over.


But not everything that ends was a waste of your time.
That's not a failure. That's a chapter closing.

Some people were meant to stay.
Some were only meant to teach you.
Both of them mattered.

You can be thankful someone was there and still let them go.
You can thank them quietly and never speak to them again.
You don't lose the good parts when you walk away.
You take them with you.

And the ones who hurt you, you don't have to fight them.
You just have to turn the page.
They stay in the chapter where they belong.


Sometimes walking away with the good memories is already enough.
So let it be finished. And go.
