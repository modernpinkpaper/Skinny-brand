The saddest part is how little it takes to save you.
Not advice. Not a speech.
Just someone saying, I didn't do it either.

You spend all night bracing for it.
You walk in with your stomach in knots.
Then one person leans over and admits they failed too.
And suddenly you can breathe again.

It happens far outside the classroom.
The friend who says, I'm behind on everything too.
The coworker who says, I have no idea what I'm doing.
The person who says, my life is a mess right now.


But notice what they never did.
They never fixed anything.
They didn't clean up your mess or hand you a plan.
That's not a solution. That's company.

And company is what you were starving for.
You don't need someone to carry your weight.
You need proof you're not the only one carrying it.
Two cups, both half empty, sitting side by side.
Not rescued. Just not alone.


So when you're the one who's behind, say it out loud.
Someone in that room needs to hear they're not the only one.
