The saddest part is that nobody ever thanked you for it.

You were the calm one. The understanding one.
You apologized first, every time.
You called it being mature.
It was just you disappearing politely.

You let the comment slide again.
You said yes when your whole body said no.
You softened your voice so nobody would call you difficult.
You carried the peace alone and called it love.

And somewhere in there you learned a quiet lie.
That being easy to love means asking for nothing.


But being the bigger person over and over is not growth.
It's just you growing smaller in a way that looks polite.
That's not kindness. That's a habit of self erasure.
Maybe the people around you are the ones who need to grow.

No is a full sentence. You don't owe anyone a paragraph.
Just because you can carry it doesn't mean you should.
You are allowed to put yourself first without guilt.
You don't have to be everyone's sunshine.


Stop living for people who never once wondered how you were doing.
