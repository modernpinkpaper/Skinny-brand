Why are you so afraid to lose someone who isn't afraid to lose you?

Sit with that for a second.
You're holding on with both hands.
They're holding on with none.

And somewhere along the way, their silence stopped feeling like a warning.
It started feeling like a test.
A test you thought you could pass if you just tried harder.

So you sent the first message again.
You explained yourself to someone who never asked.
You made yourself smaller so they'd have room to stay.
You called it patience.


But they didn't even say good morning.
That's not a puzzle. That's an answer.
Indifference isn't a door you knock on. It's a door that's already closed.

You can't love someone into wanting you.
You can't earn what should be given.
Attention you have to beg for isn't attention. It's a performance.

The right person will not need convincing.
You'll never be too much for someone who can't get enough of you.


So forgive yourself for auditioning.
Then stop showing up to a stage with no one watching.
