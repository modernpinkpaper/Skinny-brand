Just because someone called you too much doesn't mean you were.

It means they were holding something they couldn't carry.
And you believed them.
So you started making yourself smaller.

You softened your excitement before you shared it.
You apologized for texting twice.
You learned to say I'm fine when you weren't.

You began every feeling with sorry, this is stupid.
You practiced being easy to be around.
And slowly, you disappeared into it.


But you were never too much.
You were just too much for them.
That's not a flaw. That's a mismatch.

The right person doesn't ration you.
They can't get enough of you.
Your loudest laugh isn't a problem.
It's the reason they stay.

Some people find you overwhelming.
Some people find you home.
You don't have to be everyone's sunshine.
You only need the ones who don't squint.


So stop editing yourself down.
That room was always the wrong size.
You are exactly what you should be.
