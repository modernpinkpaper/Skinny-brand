There's a theory that says hope isn't what keeps people alive.
Curiosity is.
Hope asks you to believe tomorrow will be good.
And right now, you can't promise that.
So being positive feels like a lie you perform.

You say you're fine before the question even lands.
You smile so nobody asks a second time.
You cancel plans you never wanted to make.
At night you count how much longer you can carry this.
And still, morning comes and asks you to try.


But nobody said you had to be excited about the future.
You just have to be a little curious about it.
That's not optimism. That's a door left open.

You don't have to feel better. You have to keep moving.
You don't have to love the morning. You just have to see it.
Even the longest day still ends in twenty four hours.
Some people need hope. Some just need to know what happens next.


So don't force the hope.
Just stay long enough to find out.
