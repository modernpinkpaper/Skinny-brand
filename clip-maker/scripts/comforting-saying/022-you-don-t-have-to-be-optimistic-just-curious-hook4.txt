You can't force yourself to feel hopeful just because someone told you to.

Hope is a big word.
Some days it's too heavy to lift.
And the pretending gets exhausting.

You smile so people stop asking.
You say you're fine before they finish the question.
You plan a future you can't even picture yet.


But you don't have to be optimistic.
You just have to keep moving.
That's not giving up on hope.
That's lowering the bar until you can reach it.

You don't need to be excited about tomorrow.
Just a little curious about it.
Curious what the light looks like at seven.
Curious who you turn into after this.

Even the longest day only lasts twenty-four hours.
It won't hurt less today.
Maybe not tomorrow either.
But a little less, and a little less.

You're not a bowling ball.
You're allowed to land hard and still bounce.
Some days you heal. Some days you only continue.
Both of them count.


So don't aim for happy. Aim for one step.
The sun is coming up either way.
Just be there to see it.
