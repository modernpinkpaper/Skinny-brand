All of us are waiting for one kind sentence.

And some of you are still waiting.
You open a comment section like this
and you can't think of a single line.
Nobody ever said it to you.

So you read what other people were told.
The proud of you. The you matter.
You save the video to come back later.
You read their comfort like a window
you were never invited through.

Maybe the only kind words you ever got
came from a stranger in a game.
Someone you couldn't even add as a friend.
And you still remember it years later.


But think about what that means.
One sentence from a stranger held you up.
That's not proof you're too much.
That's proof you were always easy to love,
and the people around you were just quiet.

You never missed out on comfort.
Comfort missed out on you.
Some words come late. Some come from strangers.
They still count.


So let this be the line you never got.
You matter. You are worth staying for.
Keep going.
