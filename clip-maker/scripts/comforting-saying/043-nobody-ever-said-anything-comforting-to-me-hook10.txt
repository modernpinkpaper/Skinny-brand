There are some people in this world
who read other people's comfort
like a window they were never invited through.
You try to think of your own line.
And nothing comes.

Not one voice that stayed.
Maybe the only time someone said you mattered,
it was a stranger in a game.
Maybe it was one comment from someone
who will never know your name.
Maybe you cried, then felt strange for crying.


But that wasn't weakness.
That was thirst.
It's not that you're too sensitive.
It's that you went too long without.
You learned to be your own shelter.
That's why you notice kindness so fast.

The words never came because they were empty.
Not because you were unworthy.
That's not proof of your value.
That's proof of their limits.
Some people wait to be told.
Some people become the telling.


So here it is, late, from a stranger.
You matter. Someone is proud of you.
Keep going.
