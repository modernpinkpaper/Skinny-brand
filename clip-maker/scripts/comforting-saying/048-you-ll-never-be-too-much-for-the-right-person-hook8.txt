People who can't hold you don't say you're wrong.
They say you're too much.

It sounds like feedback.
It feels like a verdict.
So you start editing yourself down.

You wait longer before you text back.
You laugh quieter in rooms full of people.
You say sorry for feeling things out loud.
You call your own heart a problem.

And slowly, you get smaller.
Small enough to be kept.
Not loved. Just kept.


But too much is not a measurement.
It's a mismatch.
A cup can't hold the ocean, and the ocean was never the mistake.

You were never too loud.
They were just uncomfortable with sound.
You were never too deep.
They just stayed in the shallow end.

That's not a flaw. That's a filter.

The right person doesn't ration you.
They don't ask you to be less.
They ask you to keep going.
You'll never be too much for someone who can't get enough of you.

Some people want a mirror. Some people want sunshine.
You don't have to be everyone's sunshine.


Stop shrinking to fit a room you were never meant to stay in.
