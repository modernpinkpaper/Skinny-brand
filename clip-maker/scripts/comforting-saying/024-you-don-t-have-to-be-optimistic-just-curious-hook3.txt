The saddest part is being told to stay positive
when positive feels like a lie.

People say look on the bright side.
They say everything happens for a reason.
And you nod. And you smile.
And inside, nothing moves.

Because hope is heavy when you're tired.
You can't force yourself to feel excited
about a future you can barely picture.

You stare at the ceiling at 2 a.m.
You skip the plans you used to want.
You answer "I'm good" before anyone finishes asking.


But here's the thing nobody tells you.
You don't have to be optimistic.
You just have to be curious.

Curious about who you become after this.
Curious about a song you haven't heard yet.
Curious about a Tuesday that surprises you.

That's not hope. That's lower than hope.
And lower is easier to carry.

You don't need to believe it gets better.
You just need to stay long enough to find out.


So don't force the bright side today.
Just stay curious, and keep moving forward.
