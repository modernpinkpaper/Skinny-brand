You can't carry blame just because someone handed it to you.
You were small. They were not.
Someone needed a reason for their own mess.
And you were the easiest place to put it.
So you grew up apologizing for things you never did.

You flinch when a voice gets loud.
You say sorry before you even know why.
You still think you could have been quieter.
Easier. Better. Less trouble.
And then none of it would have happened.


But that was never yours to fix.
Nothing was broken in you.
Something was chosen by them.
You were reacting normally to things that were not normal.
That's not damage. That's you still being awake.

When a grown person makes a choice, it belongs to them.
Not to the child standing nearby.
People hurt you. That was people.
Not love. Not God. Not you.
That's not your guilt. That's their weight in your hands.


Some things are yours to heal.
The blame was never yours to keep.
So set it down.
And forgive the kid who thought it was theirs.
