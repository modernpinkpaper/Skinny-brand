How can you be grateful when you're still counting what it cost you?
Everyone tells you the same thing.
You're alive. You're lucky. Be thankful.
And you nod, because what else can you say.

But nobody asks about the nights.
The way one sound freezes your whole body.
The things you can't do the way you used to.
The people you held who didn't make it.
The person you were before it happened.
You survived, and surviving took something from you.


So here it is.
You're allowed to be angry.
Not at the people who saved you.
At what it took to be saved.
Gratitude is not rent you pay for still being here.

That's not ungrateful. That's grief.
And grief doesn't cancel out being alive.
You can be thankful and furious in the same breath.
You can cry every night for a while.
Just don't promise yourself forever.


Feel all of it.
That's how you come back.
