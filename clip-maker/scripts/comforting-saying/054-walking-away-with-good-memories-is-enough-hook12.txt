Is it really a failure if it simply finished?

We treat every ending like proof we did something wrong.
Like love only counts if it lasts forever.
So you stay long after the warmth is gone.

You keep texting someone who answers slower every week.
You defend a person who stopped choosing you.
You hold a whole friendship up with one hand.
Because leaving feels like admitting it was all a waste.


But it wasn't a waste. It was a chapter.
And chapters are supposed to end.
That's not giving up. That's finishing the page.

Some people are forever. Some people are seasons.
Both of them were real.
You can be glad someone was there,
and still not need them here.

You don't lose the good nights. You just grow past them.
They stay with you either way.
And the one who broke your trust
stays behind in the old chapter while you walk into the new one.


So take the good memories. Leave the rest.
That was always enough.
