Always trust the person who says, I didn't do the homework either.
Not the one with the perfect answer.
Not the one who tells you to try harder.
The one who sits in the mess beside you.

You forgot the assignment, and someone said they forgot too.
You said you were behind in life, and someone said, me too.
You admitted you were barely holding on, and somebody quietly nodded.
And something in your chest let go.


But look at what they never did.
They never fixed it. Nothing changed.
The work was still undone, the deadline still coming.
And somehow you could breathe again.
That's not a solution. That's company.

Shame needs you alone to survive.
Shame dies the second someone says, me too.
You didn't need advice.
You needed proof you weren't the only one failing.
Two cups don't have to be level to sit side by side.

Some people lift your weight.
Some people just stand in it with you.
And standing is enough.


So the next time you're the one falling behind, say it out loud.
Somebody near you is holding their breath, waiting to hear it.
