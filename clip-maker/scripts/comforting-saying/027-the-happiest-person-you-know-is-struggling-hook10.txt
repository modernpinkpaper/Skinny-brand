There are some people in this world who carry everyone's mood.

You're the one who makes the room lighter.
The one people call when they're falling apart.
Somewhere along the way, that stopped being a gift.
It became a job.

So you learn the small tricks.
You say fine before they finish asking.
You make the joke first, so nobody looks closer.
You listen for hours.
Then you say nothing about your own night.

And when you finally break, people get confused.
They tell you it doesn't make sense.
You're the happy one.


But a big smile isn't proof that nothing hurts.
It's proof you got very good at hiding.
That's not strength.
That's exhaustion in a nicer outfit.

You are not a mess.
You're a feeling person in a heavy world.
Some people get asked how they are.
Some are just expected to be okay.
You were never meant to live in that second group.


You don't have to be everyone's sunshine today.
Let someone else hold the light for a while.
