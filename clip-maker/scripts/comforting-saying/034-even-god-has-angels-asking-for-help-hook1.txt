There's an old saying that says even God has angels.

Think about that for a second.
The highest thing anyone can imagine
still built help into the plan.

But you decided you were the exception.
You decided your weight is too heavy for anyone.
So you carry it alone and call that strength.

You say I'm fine before they finish asking.
You cry at night with the door closed.
You answer everyone's messages and send none of your own.

And there's someone in your life right now
who would drop everything if you just said it out loud.


But you never say it.
Because somewhere you learned that needing people makes you a burden.
That's not strength. That's a cage you built yourself.

Asking is not weakness. It's trust.
You don't need someone to fix it.
You just need someone sitting next to you.
Two people, both tired, still not alone.

Some people carry you. Some people just sit with you.
Both of them count.


So tell one person one true thing today.
You were never meant to hold all of this by yourself.
