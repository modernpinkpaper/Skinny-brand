The saddest part is that you learned to call your silence strength.

Nobody taught you that on purpose.
You just noticed that people get tired.
So you started handling everything alone.

You type the long message and delete it.
You say you're fine before anyone asks twice.
You cry quietly so the house stays peaceful.

You tell yourself they already have enough going on.
You tell yourself your problem is too small.
You tell yourself you'll talk about it later.
Later never comes.


But carrying it alone was never the proof.
That's not strength. That's fear wearing strength's clothes.
Even the highest thing people pray to has help around it.
Even God has angels.

You are not a weight. You are a person.
The people who love you are not fragile.
Some friends want the easy version of you.
Some friends want the real one.
The real ones are still waiting.

Your cup can be empty next to a full one.
You don't have to match anyone to sit beside them.


So say it out loud to one person today. Just one.
