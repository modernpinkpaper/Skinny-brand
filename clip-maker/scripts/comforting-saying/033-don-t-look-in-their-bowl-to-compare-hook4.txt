You can't call your life small just because someone else looks big.
Comparison feels like measuring.
It's really just borrowing someone else's ruler.
And their ruler was never made for your life.

You scroll and see a promotion, a new place, a ring.
You look in someone's bowl to see if yours has less.
You hear about a win and feel your own good day shrink.
Every time you do it, enough moves further away.


But you were never meant to look in their bowl to compare.
You were meant to look in their bowl to see if they have enough.
That's not competing. That's caring.
And it's the only kind of looking that fills you up.

Not everyone has to be on the stage.
The ones who watch and clap matter too.
If what you do makes you happy, nobody gets to call you a failure.
That's not settling. That's knowing.
Some people live to impress a crowd. Some live to impress themselves.
Only one of those you can keep.


So stop keeping score with people who aren't playing your game.
Look down at your own bowl, and see how full it already is.
