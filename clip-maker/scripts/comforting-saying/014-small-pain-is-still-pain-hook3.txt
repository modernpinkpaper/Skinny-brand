The saddest part is how fast you say it's not that bad.

Someone else has it worse.
So you swallow it.
You call it dramatic.
You keep going like nothing happened.

You cry in the car, then fix your face before you walk in.
You say I'm fine before anyone even asks.
You tell your story like a joke so nobody worries.

But pain doesn't work on a scale.
You can drown in seven feet of water.
You can drown in twenty.
You still drown.


It's not weakness to hurt over something small.
It's honesty.
What felt like your lowest day might be someone else's normal.
That doesn't make it lighter for you.

Just because it doesn't show doesn't mean you're not bleeding.
Just because you survived doesn't mean you're not allowed to be angry.
You don't need permission to grieve.
You don't need proof that it was bad enough.

Some wounds are loud. Some are quiet.
Both still need care.


Stop waiting until it gets worse to let yourself be helped.
