Strong people don't stop hurting. They just stop mentioning it.
You learned that somewhere along the way.
That your okay was easier for everyone else.
So you got good at it.
Nobody asked twice.

You say you're fine before they finish asking.
You laugh half a second too fast.
You cry in the car, then fix your face before the door.
And people call you strong.
They say they don't know how you do it.
They never ask what it costs.


But just because it doesn't show doesn't mean you're not bleeding.
That's not strength. That's a performance.
And you've been on stage for years.

You don't have to perform to be loved.
You don't have to hold it together to be worth staying for.
Feeling a lot doesn't make you a mess.
It makes you a person in a heavy world.
You're allowed to be tired. You're allowed to be angry.
Some people need you smiling. Some can hold you crying.


Find the one who can hold it, and let your face rest.
