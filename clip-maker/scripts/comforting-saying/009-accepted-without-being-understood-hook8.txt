The people who truly love you don't need you to make sense.
They just stay.
You've spent years translating yourself.
Softer words. Smaller reactions.
Explaining your own brain like a foreign language.

You rewrite the text three times before sending it.
You apologize for talking too much about the thing you love.
You watch their face to see if you've gone too far.
You learned that being understood was the price of being kept.


But acceptance was never a reward for being explainable.
Someone can not get you and still choose you.
That's not confusion. That's love doing the work.
The right person says, I don't follow, tell me again.
Not, why are you like this.

They don't decode you. They stay curious.
Some people need a manual for you.
Some people just like the way your mind moves.
You are not too much.
You were just handed to the wrong readers.


Stop shrinking your language for people who never wanted to learn it.
