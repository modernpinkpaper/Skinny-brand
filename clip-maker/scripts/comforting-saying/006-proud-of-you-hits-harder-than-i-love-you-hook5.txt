The reason you're so good at holding it together
is that nobody ever said they were proud of you.
So you learned to carry it quietly.
You learned to clap for yourself in an empty room.
You stopped waiting to be noticed.

That's why I love you slides right off you.
Love can be a habit. Love can be a duty.
It gets said at the end of phone calls.
It gets said because someone has to.
And part of you always hears the have to.


But proud is different.
Proud means somebody watched.
They saw the nights you didn't sleep.
They saw you show up when quitting was easier.
They saw you almost give up, and not.

That's not affection. That's being seen.
Nobody owes you their pride.
They choose it. That's why it breaks you open.
And four words from a stranger can undo you.


You're still here. That's your proof.
So hear it now. I'm proud of you. Keep going.
