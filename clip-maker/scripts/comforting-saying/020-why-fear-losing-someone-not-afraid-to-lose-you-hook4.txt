You can't call it love just because you never gave up.
That's not devotion.
That's an audition.
You were proving you were worth keeping.

No good morning.
No message unless you sent it first.
You, checking your phone before your eyes opened.
You, shrinking so you'd be easier to love.

Their silence stopped feeling like an answer.
It started feeling like a test.
So you tried harder.
You confused being ignored with being challenged.


But indifference is not a challenge.
It's information.
It's not a puzzle you solve.
It's an answer you already got.
Someone who wants you doesn't make you guess.

You will never be too much for the right person.
There is no right person at the wrong time.
The time was fine. The person wasn't.
Some people are homes. Some are hallways.


So forgive yourself for chasing.
Then raise the bar and leave it there.
