The saddest part is that nobody ever said they were proud of you.

They said they loved you.
And love was easy for them to say.
Love came with being family.
Love came whether you tried or not.

But nobody watched.
Nobody noticed the nights you kept going.
Nobody saw the days you got up anyway.
So you learned to clap for yourself.
Quietly. Alone. In your own head.

Then one day a stranger typed six words.
I am proud of you, keep going.
And you sat there and cried at your screen.


Because pride is not automatic.
Nobody owes you pride.
They have to actually look at you to feel it.

I love you means you exist to me.
I'm proud of you means I saw the cost.
That's not a compliment. That's a witness.

Some people love you out of habit.
Some people see you on purpose.
And you are still here.
So don't tell me you weren't strong enough.
You already proved yourself wrong.


Go tell someone you're proud of them tonight.
It might be the first time they hear it.
