You can't be grateful on command just because you lived.

People mean well when they say it.
At least you're here. At least it wasn't worse.
And you nod. You tell them you know.
But something in your chest goes quiet.

You smile at visitors and count the minutes.
You wake up at three in the morning.
Your body still thinks it's happening.
Someone calls you lucky and you want to scream.


But gratitude is not a rule.
It's not a debt you pay for breathing.
You can be glad you're still here.
And still furious at what it cost you.
Both are true. Both are allowed.

Surviving is not the same as being okay.
That's not ingratitude. That's grief.
Anger doesn't mean you wasted the second chance.
It means you know what you almost lost.

Some feelings need fixing. Some just need room.
You don't owe anyone a brave face.
You are allowed to feel it all.


So cry every night if you need to.
Just don't plan on crying forever.
