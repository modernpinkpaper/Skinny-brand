You can't call yourself too much just because someone couldn't hold you.
That word stayed with you longer than they did.
So you started editing yourself down.
Smaller laugh. Shorter texts. Fewer feelings.

You apologized for caring too fast.
You said sorry for asking one question too many.
You learned to say I'm fine before anyone asked.
You made yourself easy to be around, and hard to find.


But you were never too much.
You were just too much for them.
That's not a flaw. That's a mismatch.
A cup overflows. The ocean doesn't.

Some people are cups. That's all.
The right person won't ration you.
They won't flinch when you feel things deeply.
They'll ask who made you question yourself.
And you'll never be too much for someone who can't get enough of you.

Not too loud. Just heard by the wrong ears.
Not too emotional. Just honest around people who weren't.
They misunderstood you and called it your problem.


You don't have to be everyone's sunshine.
Just stop dimming for people who prefer the dark.
