Is it really nothing if it still keeps you up at night?
You learned to shrink your pain before you ever said it out loud.
Someone always has it worse.
So you decide you have no right to hurt at all.

You say you're fine when you're not.
You measure your bad month against someone's bad year.
You swallow it because at least you're still here.
You wait until it gets serious enough to deserve help.
And it never feels serious enough.


But drowning in seven feet still drowns you.
Just because it doesn't show doesn't mean you aren't bleeding.
Pain isn't a contest. It's a signal.
Your lowest day might be someone's ordinary one.
That doesn't make it small. That makes it yours.
You're allowed to be angry, even when you got lucky.


Some wounds are loud, some are quiet, and both still need care.
So say it today, exactly as heavy as it feels.
