There's an old kitchen rule called the potato and the egg.

Same pot. Same heat. Same time.
The potato comes out soft.
The egg comes out hard.
Nothing changed but what was already inside.

That's why two people can lose the same thing
and walk out as two different people.
Hardship doesn't build your character.
It shows you what you already built.

You can watch it in small moments.
One person gets lied to and trusts no one again.
Another gets lied to and just gets careful.
One bad year makes someone bitter.
The same bad year makes someone wise.


But here is the part nobody tells you.
You do get a say.
Not over the water. Over the shell.

Ask what this taught you, not only what it took.
Say it out loud to one person you trust.
Stay kind in the places where it still costs you nothing.
Rest before you call yourself broken.

That's not weakness. That's training.
Hard is not the same as strong.
A bowling ball cracks. A bouncy ball comes back.


You never choose the heat.
You always choose what it makes of you.
