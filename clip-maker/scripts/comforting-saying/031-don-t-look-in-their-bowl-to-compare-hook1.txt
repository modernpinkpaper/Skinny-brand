There's an old rule about your neighbor's bowl.
Don't look in it to see if you have more.
Look in it to see if they have enough.
Most of us were taught the opposite.

So you check. Quietly. Constantly.
You measure your life against theirs.
Someone your age just bought a house.
Someone posted a trip you couldn't afford.
Someone got promoted and your good news went quiet.
You were fine ten minutes ago.


Nothing changed. Except the comparison.
But a person is not a scoreboard.
That's not motivation. That's a slow way to resent your life.
When you turn people into numbers, you stop seeing people.

Not everyone has to be on the stage.
The ones clapping matter too.
If what you're doing makes you happy, you're already winning.
Don't live to impress them.
Live so you can face yourself.
Some people are competition. Most are just people.


So look in their bowl. Not to compare.
Look to make sure they've eaten.
