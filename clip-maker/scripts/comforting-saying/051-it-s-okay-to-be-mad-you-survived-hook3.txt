The saddest part is that surviving comes with a rule.

Everyone tells you to be grateful.
Be grateful you're alive.
Be grateful it wasn't worse.
And suddenly your pain needs permission.

So you nod in the hospital bed.
You say you're lucky when people ask.
You smile at the visitors who mean well.
And then you lie awake at two in the morning,
furious, and you feel guilty for it.


But gratitude was never supposed to be a gag order.
You can be alive and still be angry.
That's not ingratitude. That's grief.

You lost something in there too.
Your body. Your calm. The person you were before.

Being spared is not the same as being okay.

You're allowed to cry every night for a while.
You're allowed to be mad it happened at all.
Some people need you to heal quietly.
You don't owe them that.


So say it out loud, even if it's ugly. It's okay to feel.
