The saddest part is you took the blame for something you never did.
You were small. You were still learning what love looked like.
And someone handed you their damage and called it yours.
So you carried it.

You apologized for taking up space.
You flinched before anyone even raised their voice.
You said sorry when nothing was your fault.
You learned to read moods like weather.
You learned that peace was something you had to earn.
You learned to shrink in rooms that should have kept you safe.


But you were reacting normally to something that was never normal.
That's not a flaw. That's survival.
Their choices were always their choices.
You didn't cause it. You couldn't have stopped it.
Not at that age. Not with that much love.

Some people hurt you and hand you the guilt.
Some places wound you and call it holy.
That was people. That was never you.
There is nothing wrong with you. There never was.


Set down what was never yours, and start forgiving yourself for surviving it.
