There are some people who look right at the part you hide.

They don't flinch.
They don't ask what happened.
They just put a hand over it.

And for one second, the mark is gone.
But you're still there.
Still breathing. Still here.

You've spent years covering it yourself.
Long sleeves in summer.
Turning your arm away from people.
Telling a story lighter than the truth.

You think the evidence makes you weak.
You think it proves you broke.


But look at what it really proves.
It's not a record of how far you fell.
It's a record of the night you stayed.

You're still here.
So don't tell me you're not strong.
You already proved yourself wrong.

Some marks are wounds.
Some are receipts.
Not damage. Survival.

And you don't have to rush.
Nobody needs you healed by Monday.
Slow is still forward.


One day let someone cover it with their hand.
Then listen to what's left.
You are.
