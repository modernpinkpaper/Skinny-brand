Just because someone says they're fine
doesn't mean they are.

You learned how to smile on time.
You learned how to answer fast.
Fine. Good. Busy. Tired, but okay.
And people believe you, because you're good at it.

You cry in the shower where it's loud.
You hold it together until the door closes.
You say you're strong because someone has to be.
And you're so tired of being the strong one.


But strong was never the same as okay.
Just because it doesn't show, doesn't mean you're not bleeding.
That's not weakness. That's a person carrying too much alone.

You don't have to perform to be loved.
You don't have to earn your rest.
You're not a mess. You're a feeling person in a messy world.

It's okay to cry. It's okay to be angry.
It's okay to be sad about something everyone says you should be grateful for.
Some people need you to be fine. Some people just need you.


So find the one you can be honest with.
And then let the mask fall.
