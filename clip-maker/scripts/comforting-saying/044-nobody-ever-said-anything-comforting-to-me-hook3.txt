The saddest part is you scrolled this far
looking for words nobody ever gave you.
Everyone else is sharing what saved them.
You sat there trying to remember one line.
And nothing came.

Not from your mom. Not from your dad.
Not from the people who were supposed to hold you.
You read their comments like a window.
A warm room you were never let into.
You told yourself you were fine with that.

Maybe the only kind words came from a stranger.
Someone online who never learned your name.
One sentence, in the middle of a game.
And you still remember it word for word.
You carry it like a coin in your pocket.


But that silence was never about you.
It's not proof you were hard to love.
It's proof they had nothing to give.
Silence is not a verdict. It's a report.
You didn't miss out because you weren't worth it.
You missed out because nobody knew how.


So take it now, late, from a stranger.
You matter. You're doing better than you think.
Save this line. It's yours to keep.
