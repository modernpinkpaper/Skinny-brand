Just because someone loves you doesn't mean they ever saw you.
Love can be automatic.
It can be a habit. A duty.
A word said fast at the end of a call.

Now think about the last time you heard the other one.
Maybe it came from a stranger under a video.
Maybe from someone who owed you nothing at all.
Maybe it never came from the people who should have said it first.

And it broke something open in you.
You sat there holding your phone, crying a little.
Over eight words from someone who barely knows you.


But that makes sense.
Love can be given by default.
Pride has to be earned in front of someone's eyes.
Pride means somebody watched.

Somebody counted the nights you almost quit and didn't.
That's not weakness. That's hunger.
You were never asking to be adored.
You were asking to be noticed.

Some people say I love you and mean I'm used to you.
Some mean I saw everything quiet you survived.


You are still here. That alone is the proof.
So hear it now. Someone is proud of you. Keep going.
