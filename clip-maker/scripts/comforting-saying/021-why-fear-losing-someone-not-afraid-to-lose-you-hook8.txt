People who want you don't make you guess.
They make it obvious.
So why are you scared to lose someone
who was never scared to lose you?

You've been treating their silence like a puzzle.
You check your phone before your eyes open.
You reread one dry message looking for warmth.
You shrink yourself so you won't seem like too much.
You call it patience. It's an audition.


But their distance was never a test.
It was an answer.
That's not a challenge. That's a choice they made.

You're not hard to love.
You're just loving someone who won't.
Someone who wants you remembers to say good morning.
The right person never finds you too much.
There's no right person at the wrong time.
The time was fine. The person wasn't.


Stop auditioning for people who already stopped watching.
