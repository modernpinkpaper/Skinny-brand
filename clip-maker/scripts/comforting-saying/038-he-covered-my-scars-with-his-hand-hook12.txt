How can you call it ugly when it's the proof you survived?
You still hide it.
Long sleeves in summer.
The photo you crop.
The story you skip over.

You tell yourself people will look at you differently.
That they'll see the worst night of your life
instead of you.
Someone covers it with their hand and says, see, it's gone.
And for one second, you breathe.
But then the hand moves.

And you're still here.
You keep waiting for the day you finally feel strong.
While forgetting what it took to stay.


But you already did the hard part.
That's not weakness written on you.
That's proof you held on.
You're not a warning.
You're the one who made it out.

Scars don't mean you broke.
They mean you healed anyway.
Some marks are damage.
Some are days you lived through.
There's no rush now.
No deadline on feeling okay.
Just the next slow, ordinary day.


So stop apologizing for the body that carried you here.
