The strangest part of the robot videos isn't the robots.

It's how badly we want them to look like us.
We give them faces. We teach them to smile.

And meanwhile, look at how we live.
We answer messages in seconds.
We skip lunch and call it focus.
We smile at people while we feel nothing at all.
We run on empty and call it being responsible.


A robot never gets tired. You do.
A robot never needs to be understood. You do.
A robot can switch off and never feel it. You can't.
That's not a weakness. That's the proof you're human.

Some places now say no humans required.
And part of you quietly wonders what you're worth.
But your worth was never your speed.

Needing rest is not a malfunction.
Feeling too much is not a defect.
Your tired is not a bug. It's information.


So let the machines be fast.
Be slow. Be soft. Be human today.
That's the one thing they're still trying to copy.
