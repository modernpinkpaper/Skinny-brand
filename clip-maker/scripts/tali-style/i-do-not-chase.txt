I do not chase.

I do not chase attention.
I do not chase people who have lost interest.
I do not chase answers from those who refuse to give them.

What is meant for me will not require begging.
Interest that is real is easy to see.

I keep my energy for the people who meet me halfway.
I save the rest for me.
