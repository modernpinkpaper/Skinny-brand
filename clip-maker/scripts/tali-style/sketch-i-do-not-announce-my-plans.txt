I do not announce my plans.

I do not share my goals before they are done.
I do not need applause for what has not happened.
I do not hand out my timeline.

People who know the plan can interfere with the plan.
Not always out of cruelty.
Sometimes out of fear.

I work quietly.
Then the results speak.
