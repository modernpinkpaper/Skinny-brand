This is how I get promoted without begging.

I do the work so well that it is hard to ignore.
I make sure the right people see it.
I put my name on what I build.
I speak with confidence and keep it short.
I never complain about the work.

I do not wait for permission.
I prepare, and I show up ready.

Opportunity follows the person who is ready.
