People treat you the way you treat yourself.

If you are always available, you become easy to ignore.
If you always say yes, your yes stops meaning anything.
If you over explain, they hear doubt.

Respect is not demanded. It is practiced.

Take your time to answer.
Let your no be a full sentence.
Walk away without an announcement.

I do not beg for what I am willing to leave.
That is how I stay valued.
