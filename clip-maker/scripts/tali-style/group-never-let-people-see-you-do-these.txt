Never let people see you do these things.

Panic when plans change.
Chase someone who left.
Explain yourself to people who have decided.
Take the bait when they want a reaction.
Share your next move before you make it.

Your calm is your power.
Your silence is your protection.

Stay steady.
Let them wonder.
