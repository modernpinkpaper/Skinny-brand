These things make people lose respect for you.

Always being available.
Saying yes to everything.
Over explaining.
Begging for what should be offered.
Staying where you are not valued.

Respect is not demanded.
Respect is practiced.

Take your time to answer.
Let no be a full sentence.
Leave without an announcement.
