You are not too nice. You have no limits.

Those are different things.

Nice people say yes before they think.
Nice people fix what is not theirs to fix.
Nice people wait for others to be fair.

They will not be fair. Some people only stop when you do.

Kindness without boundaries is just access.

I am gentle. I am also clear.
I can care about you and still say no.
Both are true.
