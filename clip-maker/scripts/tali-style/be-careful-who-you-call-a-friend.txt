Be careful who you call a friend.

Not everyone who is close to you is for you.
Some people are close to see.
Some are close to compare.
Some are close to wait for you to fall.

Watch what they do when you win.
Watch what they do when you are quiet.

Close is not the same as loyal.
Loyalty shows up on its own.
