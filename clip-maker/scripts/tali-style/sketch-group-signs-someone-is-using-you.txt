You will know when someone is using you.

They are only warm when they need something.
They know all your business and share none of theirs.
They go quiet when you win.
They disappear when you need them.
They keep score of every favor.

Do not make excuses for this.
Do not explain it away.

People show you who they are.
Believe them the first time.
