Why you are being overlooked at work.

You do great work and tell no one.
You make yourself small to keep the peace.
You wait to be noticed.
You let others present your ideas.

Nobody can value what they have never seen.
Nobody can promote what they have never heard.

Be visible.
Say your name.
Take the room you earned.
