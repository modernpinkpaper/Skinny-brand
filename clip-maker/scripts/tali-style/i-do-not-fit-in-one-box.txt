I do not fit in one box.

I am soft and I am firm.
I am quiet and I can be heard.
I am patient and I move fast.

The world loves to label you.
Labels make you easy to predict.
Predictable people are easy to handle.

I stay flexible.
I stay unreadable.
I stay free.
