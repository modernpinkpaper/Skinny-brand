I am not easy to read, and I like it that way.

I do not explain my plans.
I do not announce my goals.
I do not need a reaction to know I am on the right path.

People who know everything about you can use everything against you.
Not out of cruelty. Out of habit.

Keep your plans quiet.
Let the results speak.

I do not owe anyone a preview of my life.
Mystery is not coldness. It is protection.
