Here is how I stay hard to read.

I do not explain my plans.
I do not announce my goals.
I do not share my feelings with people who have not earned them.
I do not react to everything I hear.

The less people know, the less they can use.
Quiet is not cold.
Quiet is protection.

I keep my life mine.
