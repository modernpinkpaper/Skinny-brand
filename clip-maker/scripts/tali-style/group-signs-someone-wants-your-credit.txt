Watch for the people who want your credit.

They ask for your ideas, then present them as theirs.
They say we when it is good and you when it is bad.
They are never around while you do the work.
They always appear when it is time to be seen.

Do not argue about it.
Document your work.
Say your name in the room.

Protect what you build.
