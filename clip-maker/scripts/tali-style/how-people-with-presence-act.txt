Real presence is quiet.

They do not rush to fill the silence.
They do not chase the room.
They do not say more than they need to.

They move slowly, and people lean in.
They speak once, and people listen.
They walk in without asking if they belong.

Confidence is not loud. It is settled.

You do not have to prove you are worth noticing.
Stand still.
Let them come to you.
