You will know when someone is jealous of you.

They compliment you with a pause.
They downplay your wins.
They ask a lot of questions and give no support.
They try to compete with you quietly.
They are not happy until you are struggling.

Envy is not about you.
It is about what they feel they lack.

Keep your wins close.
Share them with those who clap.
