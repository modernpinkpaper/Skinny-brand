Being good is not enough. Be seen.

I do not hide my work and hope someone finds it.
I do not wait for permission to take up space.
I do not make myself small to keep others comfortable.

No one can choose what they have never seen.
No one can promote what they have never heard.

Visibility is a skill.
I practice it on purpose.
