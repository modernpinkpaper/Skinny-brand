The people who get noticed all do this.

They speak with confidence and keep it short.
They share their wins without apology.
They take space in a room.
They make their name easy to remember.
They show up ready, every time.

Being quiet is not the same as being humble.
Being unseen is not a virtue.

Get seen.
Then stay consistent.
