I do not move halfway.

When I decide, I decide.
I do not announce my fear.
I do not ask for permission I do not need.

Doubt is loud on the inside and invisible on the outside, until you give it a voice.
So I do not give it one.

I walk in like I belong there.
That is how I end up belonging.
