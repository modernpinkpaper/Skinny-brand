Watch what they do, not what they say.

Someone who values you does not disappear when you need them.
Someone who values you does not keep score.
Someone who values you does not ask for everything and offer nothing.

Pay attention to who is only warm when they want something.
Pay attention to who knows all your business and shares none of theirs.
Pay attention to who gets quiet the moment you win.

Kindness does not need an agenda.

You do not owe anyone access to you.
Close the door.
