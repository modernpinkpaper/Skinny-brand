Signs you have outgrown a place.

You feel drained instead of challenged.
You are the only one trying to improve.
You are tolerated instead of valued.
You have done everything you could do there.
You dread the same people every day.

Do not stay out of habit.
Everything has an expiration date.

Check it.
Then leave with grace.
