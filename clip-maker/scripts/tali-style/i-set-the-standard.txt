I do not follow the standard. I set it.

I do not wait for approval to dress how I dress.
I do not need a title to carry myself well.
I do not apologize for taking myself seriously.

The way you treat yourself is the price tag you hang on your life.
Treat yourself like you are worth it.
Others will follow your lead.
