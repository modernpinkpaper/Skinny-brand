Doing good work is not the same as being seen.

Nobody can value what they do not know about.
Nobody can promote what they have never heard.
Nobody can choose you if you stay out of sight.

Stop waiting to be noticed.
Stop hoping your effort speaks for itself.
Stop making yourself small to keep the peace.

The quiet ones are not forgotten because they are weak.
They are forgotten because they are hidden.

Be visible.
Take up the room you earned.
