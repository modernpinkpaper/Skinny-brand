I let people reveal themselves.

I do not interrogate.
I do not rush to conclusions.
I do not need every answer today.

I give it time and I listen.
People get comfortable.
Comfortable people tell the truth with their behavior.

All I have to do is pay attention.
Everything else is already on display.
