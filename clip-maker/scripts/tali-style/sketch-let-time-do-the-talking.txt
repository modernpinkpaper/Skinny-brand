Let time do the talking.

I do not rush to prove anything.
I do not defend myself to people who are not listening.
I let the days pass and the truth show up.

The more I sit still, the more I see.
I see their habits.
I see their patterns.
I see who stays and who fades.

Time exposes everyone.
I just wait.
