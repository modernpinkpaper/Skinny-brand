These are the habits that protect my peace.

I do not check what people think of me.
I do not reply when I am upset.
I do not carry things from last year.
I do not discuss my plans with people who doubt me.
I do not stay in rooms that shrink me.

Peace is not luck.
It is a set of rules.

I follow them every day.
