# The formula: how to talk like this on any topic

This is built from the top 20 Philosophaire videos (see `01-top-20-study.md`).
Use it to write your own scripts. Do not copy their lines — copy the **shape**.

## The numbers to hit

| Thing | Target |
|---|---|
| Video length | 61–70 seconds (at least 61: TikTok only pays for videos of 60 seconds or more) |
| Script length | 155–180 words |
| Speaking speed | slow, about 2.5 words per second |
| Average sentence | about 8–10 words |
| Very short sentences (5 words or less) | about 1 in 4 |
| "you" / "your" | the most used word. Talk **to** the viewer |
| "I" | almost never |

## The 6-part structure

```
1. HOOK        (0–4s,   1 sentence)   A full thought that stings or surprises.
2. OPEN IT UP  (4–15s,  2–3 lines)    Say why it's true / why it hurts.
3. MAKE IT REAL(15–30s, list of 3)    Small real examples the viewer has lived.
4. THE TURN    (30–40s, 1–2 lines)    "But..." / "That's not X. That's Y."
5. PUNCH LINES (40–55s, 3–5 short)    Short quotable lines. Rhythm.
6. CLOSE       (55–65s, 1 line)       One line to save/share. Often a gentle order.
```

## 13 hook templates (fill in the blanks)

Pick the one that fits your topic best.

1. **Named idea:** "There's a [Japanese / old / psychology] [theory / legend / rule] called ___."
2. **Named idea (short):** "There's a theory that says ___."
3. **Sad truth:** "The saddest part is ___."
4. **Hard rule:** "You can't ___ just because ___."
5. **Flattering reason:** "The reason you're so good at ___ is because ___."
6. **Ladder:** "If you ___, that's ___. If you ___, that's ___. But if ___, that's ___."
7. **Blunt line (2–4 words):** "___ is weird." / "You eventually ___."
8. **Expose a type:** "[Type of person] don't ___. They ___."
9. **We are all:** "All of us are ___."
10. **Rare people:** "There are some people in this world who ___."
11. **Trust a group:** "Always trust the ___."
12. **Deep question:** "How can you ___ when ___?" / "Is it really ___ if ___?"
13. **Not the same:** "Just because someone ___ doesn't mean ___."

## The speaking tricks (use 4–6 per script)

- **Not X. Y.** — "It's not a loss. It's protection." (used in almost every video)
- **Groups of three** — "Time doesn't matter. Distance doesn't matter. ___ doesn't matter."
- **Same start, repeated** — "Maybe... Maybe... Maybe..." / "They don't... They don't..."
- **The flip** — swap two words: "Not the one who's always around, but the one who's always there."
- **Name the viewer's thought** — "Part of you still believes ___."
- **Small real details** — "a random night in June", "conversations at 2 a.m."
- **Soft labels** — "Some people are chapters. Some are mirrors."
- **"That's not X, that's Y"** — "That's not love. That's convenience."
- **Answer inside the doubt** — "If you're asking where you stand, that's your answer."
- **One old wise voice (optional)** — a philosopher, a proverb, "psychology says".
- **Fragments** — "No closure. No final talk. Just a quiet fade."
- **End with a gentle order** — "Trust the detour." "Hold on to them." "Just keep going."

## Tone rules

- Calm, slow, sure. Like a friend talking late at night.
- Simple words. A 10-year-old should understand every word.
- Kind to the viewer, a little hard on "them" or on life.
- No jokes, no hype, no "hey guys", no call to follow.
- The feeling should go: **seen → hurt a little → understood → hopeful or wiser.**


## Worked example (new topic: quitting a job you hate)

> **Hook:** Is it really safe if it's slowly making you smaller?
>
> We call the job we hate "stable". We call the fear "being smart". We tell
> ourselves, just one more year. Then another. Then another.
>
> But you already know. You feel it on Sunday nights. You feel it when you
> smile at people who don't see you. You feel it every time someone asks,
> "How's work?" and you say "fine" too fast.
>
> That's not comfort. That's a habit. And a habit is not the same as a
> choice.
>
> Leaving might be scary. Staying is scary too. It's just quieter.
>
> Some places are a home. Some are a waiting room. You were never meant to
> live in a waiting room.
>
> So if you're going to be afraid either way, be afraid while moving
> forward.

(About 130 words. Add one or two lines to reach ~60 seconds.)

## Making the video

1. **Voice:** record slow and low. Pause after each key line. Or use a calm
   AI voice at about 0.9x speed.
2. **Music:** soft sad piano or ambient bed, quiet under the voice. Use
   TikTok's library so the sound is allowed.
3. **Layout:** 1080x1920 black background. Put the clip in the middle as a
   horizontal box (about 1080x800).
4. **Visuals:** one moody clip every 3–6 seconds that matches the line (rain,
   a person alone, sunset, city lights, a window, a train). Philosophaire uses
   clips from famous films and cartoons — that can get videos muted or
   removed for copyright. Safer: your own footage, stock footage (Pexels,
   Pixabay), or AI-made animated scenes in one soft style.
5. **Look:** add film grain, a soft glow, a little fade and dark edges.
   (CapCut: "Film" / "VHS" / "Grain" filters.)
6. **Captions:** 1–3 words at a time, white, small clean font, light shadow,
   in the center of the clip. (CapCut auto captions → set to "1–3 words per
   line".)
7. **Post:** no need for a long caption. The top videos have none.

## Quick checklist before you post

- [ ] The hook works alone as a quote.
- [ ] "You" is in almost every line.
- [ ] There's a list of 3 real examples.
- [ ] There's a clear "But..." turn.
- [ ] At least 2 "Not X. Y." lines.
- [ ] The last line is something people would screenshot.
- [ ] 61–70 seconds, slow voice, 1–3 words on screen.

## Output format for the video maker

- One short line per clip, about 4–10 words.
- A blank line between thoughts. Two blank lines = a longer pause (use before THE TURN and before the CLOSE).
- Plain words only: no part labels (HOOK:, CLOSE:...), brackets, stars, emojis or stage directions.
- Put the whole script in one code block so I can copy it.
