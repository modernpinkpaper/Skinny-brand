# Style guide: @connectingherdots (Tali)

Built from her whole account (370 transcripts in `research/transcripts-connectingherdots/`). Use the **shape**, not her lines.

## What the numbers say
- **"I" mode wins.** 31 of her top 50 videos are "I" mode (about 62%). In her weakest 150, it is 101 of 150 (67%), so "I" mode alone is not the secret. The top ones are also the **shortest and flattest**.
- Her two biggest videos (1.5M and 1.1M views) are 36 and 90 words. Median video: 72 words.
- Her top openers: "I am...", "I don't...", "I do not...", "I live...", "I move...".
- Her "you" videos that do well are one flat order or one hard truth, not a list ("You know immediately when someone doesn't like you. Don't play games with yourself. Get out of there ASAP.").
- Rule: **shorter and flatter beats longer and clever.** Aim for 40 to 90 words.


## What she sounds like
- Calm, sure, unhurried. She never explains, argues or hedges. She states it, like it is already true.
- Plain words. No slang, no filler ("like", "honestly"), no questions, no jokes.
- Short sentences, about 6 to 12 words. Many are 3 to 6 words ("I go easy on me." / "Let things go.").
- Every video is ONE idea, said several ways. No list of tips, no story.

## Two modes (pick one per video, do not mix)
1. **"I" mode (identity).** She talks as the person she wants the viewer to become.
   "I do not hold on to anything longer than necessary." The viewer borrows the lines.
2. **"You" mode (orders).** Firm and kind, like a friend who has had enough.
   "Stop requiring people to be who you want them to be."

## The moves she repeats
| Move | Example from her videos |
|---|---|
| Same opener 3 to 4 times in a row | "I do not... I do not... I do not..." / "Stop... Stop... Stop..." |
| Then a different, flatter line to break it | "This is my life." / "Let things go." |
| A reason that sounds like a fact | "Freedom is a gateway to success." |
| "Not X, it's Y" | "The hardest thing to be is happy." |
| Power is control, privacy, ease, speed | "No one has enough access to me to disrupt my life." |
| Ends on the shortest, firmest line | "I go easy on me." / "Take better control of your life." |

## Shape of a script
```
1. FIRST LINE   one flat, bold statement (the whole idea)
2. SAY IT AGAIN 3 to 5 lines, same opener, each a new angle
3. THE REASON   1 to 2 lines, stated as fact
4. THE CLOSE    1 short line. No call to action in the script (use the caption for that).
```
Length: 70 to 130 words (30 to 55 seconds at the slow voice). Shorter is fine.
Do not name any book, author or numbered law. Say the idea itself.
One line = one clip. A blank line = a pause. Blank line only before the reason and before the close.
