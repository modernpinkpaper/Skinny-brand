There’s a particular kind of hurt
that comes from having a parent who could have been there…
and just wasn’t.

And for a long time,
you might keep wondering what you could’ve done differently.

Maybe if you were easier.
Maybe if you needed less.
Maybe if you accomplished more.
Maybe then they would’ve shown up.

But you were the child.

You were never supposed to earn being cared for.

And I hope one day you realize
that their absence doesn’t get to become the story of your life.

Because you’re going to meet people who stay.

People who remember the little things.
People who check on you.
People who celebrate you.
People who make room for you without making you feel like a burden.

And slowly, you’ll build a life filled with a different kind of love.

Not the love you had to chase.

The kind that comes toward you.

You may always wish they had been different.

But one day, that wish won’t hurt quite as much.

Because you’ll look around at the life you built
and realize…

someone not showing up for your beginning
never meant you couldn’t have a beautiful rest of the story.
