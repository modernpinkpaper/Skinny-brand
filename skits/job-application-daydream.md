# Skit: the job-application daydream (original)

**Theme:** the daydream you have the second you hit "Submit" on a job application, then the reality.
**Style:** one person plays everyone. Phone camera, one room, natural light. Big faces. The same text on screen the whole time. A long fantasy, a short quiet reality.
**Length:** about 60 seconds. **Props:** a phone, a hair tie. (A bag is optional.)
**Rule for the viewer:** hair DOWN = real life. Hair UP = the daydream.

**Text on screen, from the first frame to the last:**
> me, 4 seconds after I hit submit on a job application

---

## 0:00 – 0:05 REAL LIFE (hair down)
Close-up. She looks at her phone, flat face. She taps the screen.
She reads it out, deadpan: **"Application received."**
One slow blink. She snaps a hair tie, pulls her hair up into a ponytail. (This is the switch.)

## 0:05 – 0:53 THE DAYDREAM (hair up, big face, looks left and right at people who aren't there)

**The call (0:05)**
Phone buzzes. She answers instantly, super calm and professional.
**"Hello? Yes, this is she. Tomorrow at nine? I'm free."**
She covers the phone and whispers to the camera: **"I'm always free."**

**The interview (0:12)**
Looks left at the boss, leans in, serious.
**"Where do I see myself in five years? Honestly? Your chair."**
Laughs at her own joke. Boss laughs too. She points at him, nodding.
**"I'm kidding. Mostly."**
She listens, eyes widening.
**"Monday? You're sure? Oh, you're so kind."**

**Day one (0:24)**
She looks right at a new coworker, big friendly smile.
**"Hi! I'm so happy to be here. I brought snacks for everyone."**
She leans toward the camera, half whisper: **"Dana already loves me."**
She turns to Dana. **"No, I don't mind staying late. I love staying late."**

**Payday (0:34)**
She opens an imaginary envelope, gasps, holds it to her chest.
**"Okay, don't panic. I'm just going to treat myself. A little treat. A tiny apartment with a balcony."**

**The promotion (0:42)**
The boss says something. She shakes her head, hand on chest.
**"Manager? After two weeks? No, no, it's too much."** Beat. **"Okay. Fine."**
She stands, holds an imaginary glass, clears her throat.
**"I'd like to thank the application I submitted four seconds ago."**
She wipes a tear. Looks at Dana. **"Dana, don't cry, you're making me cry."**

## 0:53 – 1:03 REAL LIFE (hard cut, hair down again)
Same close-up as the opening. Phone in hand. Phone buzzes.
She reads the email in her head. Face drops slowly.
Silent beat: lips pressed together. A small nod. Side-eye to the camera.
(No words. The comments will finish the joke.)

**Last 3 seconds, the loop:** she sighs, taps the phone, and the screen shows a new application page.
She pulls the hair tie out of her pocket and snaps it onto her wrist. Cut.
(The video ends exactly where it began, so people want to watch it again.)
