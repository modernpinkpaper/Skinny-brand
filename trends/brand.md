# Brand profile (the trend filter reads this file; edit it any time)

Page: @theskinnylaws. Calm, kind, "friend talking late at night" voice. Audience: mostly women who want
to feel better in their own life: healing, self-love, relationships and family, burnout, habits, gentle
healthy living (food, sleep, movement, mindset). Videos are 60+ second voiceovers built on a topic the viewer
feels "seen" by (see clip-maker/FORMULA.md). The paid product is The Skinny Laws Vault (healthy habits).

Good fit: feelings people relate to, everyday habits, health and wellness, food and cooking, sleep, stress,
self-worth, friendships, family, money stress, glow-ups and routines, seasonal moods (fall, new month, Monday).
Weak fit: sports scores, celebrity gossip, product launches, tech, finance/stocks, games.

Never use (hard skip): tragedies, deaths, disasters, shootings, wars, crimes, politics and politicians,
elections, and real people's drama (breakups, feuds, scandals, lawsuits). No extreme dieting or weight-loss
claims. Scripts must be original; never copy another creator's words.
